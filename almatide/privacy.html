AlmaTide Privacy Policy

Version 1, October 2026. AlmaTide is an astrology app (birth chart and moon calendar) for entertainment and reflection, for adults 18 and older, made by Amg Up.

The short version

Everything you type stays on your phone. AlmaTide has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We cannot see your data, and we never sell or share it. AlmaTide does not ask for your location: you type your birth place yourself.

What AlmaTide keeps (only on your phone)

In the private storage of the app, only what you type or choose:

  • Your birth data: your birth date, time and place, and the chart worked out from them.
  • The people you add: their names and birth data. Add only people who are OK with it. Their data stays on this phone.
  • Your saved days, with the notes you write, and the Trash.
  • Your alerts: the sky events you pick (full moon, new moon, Mercury retrograde) and the hour. They are scheduled on the phone.
  • Your settings: language, theme, motion, the date your free trial started, plus a few internal counters that decide when to show a card. The counters never leave the phone.

What AlmaTide does not keep: your contacts, photos, location, card numbers or any account. AlmaTide never asks for them.

Places and time zones

The list of cities comes inside the app (GeoNames, CC BY 4.0) and the time zone rules come from your phone. Looking up a place does not use the internet.

When it leaves your phone

Only when you do it: when you share a picture of your chart or of the sky, make a PDF, or save a backup file (.json) or a spreadsheet file (CSV), it goes to the app or place you choose, and the rules of that place apply from there. We do not receive it. The backup file and the CSV are kept by you, wherever you choose to save them. If Android backup is on in your phone, Android may keep a copy of AlmaTide's data in your own Google account; you control that in your phone's settings, and we cannot read it.

Purchases

The yearly subscription and the one-time purchase are made through Google Play, under Google's own privacy policy. AlmaTide only learns whether the purchase is active. To make purchases work, the Google Play library connects to Google and may send technical data about the purchase and the phone; it never receives your birth data or your people.

Permissions

Notifications: only for the sky alerts you turn on. Restart: to set your alerts again after the phone restarts. Vibration: for alerts and for button feedback. Google Play purchases and internet: used only for the purchase; the pages of the app never load anything from the internet. Saving or restoring a backup uses the file picker of Android, so AlmaTide needs no storage permission and reads only the file you pick. AlmaTide does not use location, contacts or camera.

Other apps

The link to rate AlmaTide only opens a Google Play page, and the share buttons use the sharing menu of Android. They send no data from AlmaTide on their own.

Deleting your data

You can delete each person and each saved day in the app (they go to the Trash, and you can empty the Trash in Settings). Uninstalling the app deletes everything from your phone (a backup file you saved yourself stays where you put it). Because we do not have your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Adults only

AlmaTide is for adults 18 and older. It is not meant for children.
