ApartKit Privacy Policy

Version 1.1, November 2026. ApartKit is an envelope budget notebook for adults 18 and older, made by Amg Up.

The short version

Your envelopes stay on your phone. ApartKit has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics, and it does not connect to any bank. We cannot see your data, and we never sell or share it.

What ApartKit keeps

Only what you type: your envelopes, amounts, notes and dates, your tanda and your family sends, your settings and the trash. It is kept in the private storage of the app on your phone.

When it leaves your phone

Only when you do it: when you save or share a backup file or a spreadsheet (CSV), it goes to the app or place you choose, and the rules of that place apply from there. If the backup of your phone is on, Android keeps a copy in your own Google backup only when it is encrypted on your phone; we cannot read it.

Purchases

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google Play receives what it needs for the purchase under its own privacy policy; it never receives your envelopes. To make purchases work, the Google Play library connects to Google and may send technical data about the purchase and the phone.

Permissions

Notifications, only if you turn on a reminder. Restart, to set your reminders again after the phone restarts. Fingerprint, only if you turn on the lock. Internet, used only by Google Play for purchases.

Deleting your data

You can delete entries and envelopes in the app at any time. Settings > Delete all my data deletes everything from this phone: envelopes, expenses, money in, the trash, the daily copies, your settings, the lock and the reminders. Uninstalling the app also deletes it. Because we do not have your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom

ApartKit processes no personal data on any server, so there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on our side: everything is on your phone and you control it there (see Deleting your data). Google Play is the controller of your purchase data, under the Google privacy policy. If you still have a question about your rights, write to the address below.

Accessibility

ApartKit works with TalkBack, large text and reduce motion. If something does not work for you with TalkBack or large text, write to the address below and we will fix it.

Adults only

ApartKit is for adults 18 and older. It is not meant for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with its date, and the app will show a short notice.
