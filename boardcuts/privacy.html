Who we are

Board Cuts is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means Amg Up. Privacy contact: pedalup180@gmail.com.

Short version

Board Cuts is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. It has no account, no servers for your data, no ads and no analytics. What you write stays on your phone.

A backup, CSV, PDF or drawing file leaves your phone only when you save or share it yourself. Payments are handled by Google Play.

What Board Cuts stores on your phone

You pick a furniture plan, type your sizes and get the drawing, the cut list, the sheet layout and the shopping list. Board Cuts stores the projects you create (sizes, cut lists, notes and status), your list of sheets and boards with the prices you type, an optional logo for the PDF, an optional small photo of each finished piece, your units, theme and language, and the date you first opened the app. It lives in the private storage of the app. We do not receive it.

What leaves your phone only when you choose

A backup file, a CSV file, a PDF or a picture of a drawing that you save or share with the Android menu (a backup also carries the logo you picked and the photos of your projects). You pick where it goes. The logo and the photos you pick are copied into the private storage of the app (the photo is made small and kept inside the project) and only leave with the PDF, the picture or the backup you share. The app uses the system photo picker, so it never asks for access to your photos. The message you send when you recommend the app to a friend goes through the app you choose (for example WhatsApp), not through us.

Android automatic backup

If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may copy the app files to your Google account, encrypted. This is a setting of your phone and Google, not of Board Cuts. It never includes purchases or an unfinished draft.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. Board Cuts only learns from Google Play whether Pro is active. The Google Play Billing library talks to Google for that; Board Cuts does not send your projects.

Age signals

When the app opens and before a purchase, the app may ask Google Play, through the official Play Age Signals library, whether the account belongs to an adult. The answer is used at that moment and is not stored or sent anywhere by Board Cuts.

Permissions

Internet and network state: used by the Google Play libraries for purchases. Board Cuts does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or your files.

Adults only

Board Cuts is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can view, export, back up and delete it from Settings, or by uninstalling the app. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to pedalup180@gmail.com with any question; since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new date appears at the top. Important changes are announced in the app or in Google Play.

Contact

Questions about privacy: pedalup180@gmail.com
