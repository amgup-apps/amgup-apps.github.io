Cattle Book Privacy Policy

Version 3, November 16, 2026. Cattle Book is an app for keeping cattle records on your phone, for adults 18 and older, made by Amg Up.

The short version

Everything you type and every photo you take stays on your phone. Cattle Book has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We cannot see your data, and we never sell or share it.

What Cattle Book keeps (only on your phone)

In the private storage of the app, only what you type, take or choose:

  • Your animals: tag number, color, name, sex, class, birth date, breed, mother and sire, brand or mark, the official ID exactly as you type it, pasture and notes.
  • Your records for each animal: weights, treatments (product, reason, dose, route, lot, who gave it, vet, the meat and milk times you type, next dose and notes), breedings, preg checks, calvings, moves, sales, deaths and notes.
  • Your pastures, your list of products and your sales (buyer name, price, weight and head you type).
  • Your ranch name and settings, such as language, units, gestation days, the daily reminder and field options.
  • Photos of animals, if you add them.

Cattle Book stores what you type. It does not check, calculate or confirm anything about withdrawal times.

Photos

You take a photo with the camera of your phone or pick one with the Android photo picker. Cattle Book does not ask for the camera or photos permission. Each photo is saved smaller in the private storage of the app. Photos do not leave your phone unless you make a backup file that includes them.

PDFs, backups, spreadsheets and files for another phone

Cattle Book makes a PDF, a backup file (.zip), a spreadsheet (CSV) or a file for another phone only when you ask, and it goes where you choose (WhatsApp, email, Drive, Files, print). From there the policy of that service applies. A file for another phone leaves out sale prices and buyers unless you turn that on. Combining files is not live sync: each phone keeps its own copy.

Android backup

If you turned on Android backup, Android may save the data of the app (without photos, and only up to its own size limit) in your own Google account. We do not receive any of it. Your backup file is the real backup: make one now and then.

Purchases

After 30 free days with no card, adding new records, working a group, importing a spreadsheet and combining another phone's file need one payment: US$39.99, one payment; Google Play shows it in your currency, with no subscription. Google Play handles the payment. Cattle Book never sees your card. The app only learns whether it was purchased.

Reminders and permissions

The daily reminder is scheduled by your phone and only repeats dates and doses you typed. Cattle Book uses these permissions: notifications (to show it), restart (to schedule it again after the phone restarts), Google Play billing, and internet and network state (only for Google Play to answer the purchase and the review). Cattle Book does not ask for location, contacts, microphone, camera, storage, photos or exact alarms.

Children

Cattle Book is made for adults and is not for children.

Age signals

When the app opens and before a purchase the app may ask Google Play, through the official Play Age Signals library, whether the account belongs to an adult. The answer is used at that moment and is not stored or sent anywhere by Cattle Book.

Deleting your data

You can delete a record, or delete all data in Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data from the phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you saved them.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can see it, export it, back it up and delete it there. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to us with any question; since we hold no personal data of yours, there is nothing we can look up, correct or delete on our side.

Contact

Questions: pedalup180@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.

Cattle Book keeps the records you type. It is not connected to any government system. It is not an official record, a health certificate or a bill of sale, and it does not give veterinary advice.
