DayKick Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Leer en español

DayKick is a routine timer for Android: it walks you through your morning (or any) routine one step at a time, with alarms that ring even with the screen off. It is made by Amg Up, an independent developer; in this policy, “we” means Amg Up. This page explains what the app does with your data, in plain words.

The short version

  • DayKick does not collect, store on servers or share any data. Your routines, history and settings stay on your phone.
  • There is no account, no ads and no analytics. DayKick has no server.
  • Pro purchases go through Google Play. DayKick never sees your payment details.
  • DayKick does not collect consumer health data.

Contact: pedalup180@gmail.com

What DayKick stores on your phone

DayKick keeps these files in its private app folder, which other apps cannot read:

  • your routines (names, steps, minutes, emoji, start time, days, leave-by time);
  • your history: the days you showed up and how each routine went (start, end, minutes, steps skipped);
  • your settings (language, look, sounds, vibration, the night-before review time);
  • routines you deleted, kept for 30 days in "Recently deleted" so you can restore them (then removed), and drafts you did not save yet;
  • the date your 30-day trial started and the last Pro status that Google Play reported (never your payment details);
  • the routine that is running right now, so it can continue after the phone restarts.

If you turn on Android backup, Android may copy these files (except the running routine) to your Google account. That copy is made by Android, under your Google account settings, not by DayKick.

You can also save your own backup file (.json) or export your history (CSV) with the system file picker, and keep them wherever you like. These files never leave your phone through DayKick.

Voice (optional)

While the app is open, DayKick can read the current step out loud with the text-to-speech engine of your phone (the one you chose in Android settings). You can turn it off in Settings. DayKick does not use the microphone, does not record audio and sends nothing; the speech engine is part of your phone and works under its own terms.

Alarms and notifications

DayKick uses alarms and notifications only to ring at the end of each step, at the start time you chose, for the optional night-before review, and to show a silent notification with the current step while a routine runs (its name, the time it ends, and the Done and +1 min buttons). They are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere. The silent notification makes no sound; depending on the lock screen setting of your phone, the names of your steps can show on the lock screen. Android asks your permission for notifications (Android 13 and newer) and for exact alarms ("Alarms and reminders").

Health data

DayKick does not ask for, store or infer health data. The names of your steps and the times of your routines are not health data, and they never leave your phone.

Consumer health data (Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada and similar laws): DayKick does not collect consumer health data.

Purchases (Pro)

DayKick has a 30-day trial inside the app (no card, it never renews by itself) and an optional Pro upgrade (monthly, yearly or lifetime) through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the payment and may process purchase data under the Google Play terms. DayKick never sees your payment details and does not keep your purchase history: it only learns from Google Play whether Pro is active on your phone. The Google Play Billing library that DayKick uses may send technical data about the purchase service to Google, under the Google Play terms; DayKick itself sends nothing.

Internet

The DayKick screens never go online. The only connections are the ones Google Play makes for purchases and for its review window, if you choose to use it.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Delete everything in the app, delete your routines, clear the DayKick storage in the Android settings, or uninstall DayKick: that deletes everything on your phone. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you saved them. Delete everything also turns off the alarms. It does not cancel a subscription or remove a purchase: those belong to your Google account, and you manage them in Google Play.

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom

DayKick processes no personal data on any server, so there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on our side: everything is on your phone and you control it there (see "Deleting your data"). Google Play is the controller of your purchase data, under the Google privacy policy. If you still have a question about your rights, write to the address below.

Accessibility

DayKick follows the system font size, works with TalkBack and never uses color alone to say something. If something does not work for you, write to pedalup180@gmail.com.

Children

DayKick is meant for adults (18 and older) and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and the "Last updated" date will change.
