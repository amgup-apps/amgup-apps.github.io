Day Sorted Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026 · Leer en español

Who we are

Day Sorted is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means Amg Up. Privacy contact: pedalup180@gmail.com.

Short version

Day Sorted is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. It has no account, no servers for your data, no ads and no analytics. What you write stays on your phone.

A backup or CSV file, or a photo, leaves your phone only when you save or share it yourself. Your photos never leave your phone on their own. Payments are handled by Google Play.

What Day Sorted stores on your phone

You plan your day with tasks, notes, a calendar and a focus timer, and keep everything on your phone. Day Sorted stores your tasks, lists, notes, events, time entries, settings and the photos you add. It lives in the private storage of the app. We do not receive it.

What leaves your phone only when you choose

A backup file or a CSV file that you save or share with the Android menu. You pick where it goes. The message you send when you recommend the app to a friend goes through the app you choose (for example WhatsApp), not through us.

Android automatic backup

If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may copy the app files to your Google account, encrypted. This is a setting of your phone and Google, not of Day Sorted. It never includes purchases or an unfinished draft.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. Day Sorted only learns from Google Play whether Pro is active. The Google Play Billing library talks to Google for that; Day Sorted does not send your tasks or notes.

Age signals

When the app opens and before a purchase, the app may ask Google Play, through the official Play Age Signals library, whether the account belongs to an adult. The answer is used at that moment and is not stored or sent anywhere by Day Sorted.

Permissions

Notifications and exact alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS and SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): used only for the reminders and timers you set, so they arrive on time. Restart receiver (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): puts your reminders back after the phone restarts. Phone calendar (READ_CALENDAR): optional, read only, asked inside the Calendar screen; those events are shown on your screen and are not saved, backed up or sent anywhere. Internet and network state: used by the Google Play libraries for purchases. Photos: you add them with the Android photo picker or the system camera, so the app needs no camera or storage permission, and they stay on your phone. Day Sorted does not ask for location, contacts or microphone.

Adults only

Day Sorted is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can view, export, back up and delete it from Settings, or by uninstalling the app. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to pedalup180@gmail.com with any question; since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new date appears at the top. Important changes are announced in the app or in Google Play.

Contact
