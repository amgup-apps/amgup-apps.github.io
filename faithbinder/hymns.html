Hymn rights table

Faith Binder · Amg Up · updated November 17, 2026 · Faith Binder

Every hymn in Faith Binder was checked one by one against this rule (rule FB-H). A hymn is included only if: (1) it was published before 1931; (2) every person who made it (words, music, translation) died in 1945 or earlier; (3) if the author is unknown, it was first published in 1930 or earlier; (4) the words are taken from an edition printed before 1931, never from a modern hymnal; (5) the melody is the original one (the soprano line of the old setting), never a modern arrangement.

English: 158 hymns (146 with melody), from the Open Hymnal Project transcriptions and, where noted, checked against Gospel Hymns No. 5 with Standard Selections. Spanish: 36 hymns from El Nuevo Himnario Evangélico (New York, 1915). The names of old hymnals below are only sources; Faith Binder belongs to no church.

If you believe a hymn here is still under copyright, write to pedalup180@gmail.com and we will review it and remove it in the next version if needed.

Tabla de derechos de los himnos (en español)

Cada himno de Faith Binder se revisó uno por uno con esta regla (regla FB-H): entra solo si (1) se publicó antes de 1931; (2) todas las personas que lo hicieron (letra, música, traducción) murieron en 1945 o antes; (3) si el autor es desconocido, se publicó por primera vez en 1930 o antes; (4) la letra se toma de una edición impresa antes de 1931, nunca de un himnario moderno; (5) la melodía es la original. Si crees que un himno tiene dueño, escríbenos a pedalup180@gmail.com.
