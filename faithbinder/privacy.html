Faith Binder Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Leer en español

Who we are

Faith Binder is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means Amg Up. Privacy contact: pedalup180@gmail.com.

Short version

Faith Binder is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. It has no account, no servers for your data, no ads and no analytics. The app does not connect to the internet: your notes, prayers and recordings stay on your phone.

A backup file, a sermon note, a verse image, a Sunday list or your Year card leaves your phone only when you save or share it yourself. Payments are handled by Google Play.

What Faith Binder stores on your phone

You read the Bible, keep sermon notes, a prayer list and memory verses, and sing classic hymns, all on your phone. Faith Binder stores your highlights and notes, reading progress, memory verses, sermon notes, prayer requests, hymn lists and settings (and sermon recordings, if you make them). It lives in the private storage of the app. We do not receive it. The Bible texts, hymns and melodies are inside the app.

No internet connection

Faith Binder makes no network requests of its own. The only connection is the one Google Play makes for the purchase and for the review window.

What leaves your phone only when you choose

A backup file, a sermon note as text or PDF, a verse image, the Sunday list, your Year card or Year in review PDF, or a recording, when you save or share it with the Android menu. You pick where it goes. Images, lists and the Year card never include your prayers or your notes; the Year in review PDF includes answered prayers only if you switch that on.

The message you send when you recommend the app to a friend goes through the app you choose (for example WhatsApp), not through us.

Android automatic backup

If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may copy the text data of the app (highlights, notes, plans, memory verses, sermon notes, prayers, hymn lists and settings) to your Google account, encrypted. It never includes sermon recordings, purchases or an unfinished draft. This is a setting of your phone and Google, not of Faith Binder.

Microphone and recordings

The microphone is used only when you tap Record in a sermon note, after a short explanation. The audio is saved in the private storage of the app and is not sent anywhere. Recordings are not in the backup file or in Android backup.

Lock, reminders and voice

If you turn on the lock, the app asks for the screen lock of your phone (fingerprint, face or PIN) through Android. The app never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN. While the lock is on, the app hides its content from screenshots and from the recent apps screen.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your phone; nothing is sent to a server. Reading aloud uses the text-to-speech voice of your phone, set by Android; Faith Binder does not send the text anywhere.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. Faith Binder only learns from Google Play whether Pro is active. The Google Play Billing library talks to Google for that; Faith Binder does not send your notes or prayers.

Age signals

When the app opens and before a purchase, the app may ask Google Play, through the official Play Age Signals library, whether the account belongs to an adult. The answer is used at that moment and is not stored or sent anywhere by Faith Binder.

Permissions

Internet and network state: used only by the Google Play libraries for the purchase. Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): only for the reminders you turn on. Restart receiver (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): puts your reminders back after the phone restarts. Biometric (USE_BIOMETRIC): to unlock the app with the screen lock of your phone. Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): only to record a sermon when you tap Record. Faith Binder does not ask for location, contacts, camera, photos or storage permissions; backups go through the Android picker only when you choose.

Age

Faith Binder is for people 13 and older. It is not made for children and we do not knowingly collect data from children; the app collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can view, export, back up and delete it from Settings, or by uninstalling the app. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to pedalup180@gmail.com with any question; since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new date appears at the top. Important changes are announced in the app or in Google Play.

Contact
