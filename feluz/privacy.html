FeLuz Privacy Policy

Version 3, November 2026. FeLuz is a Catholic prayer app (rosary, novenas, intentions and a prayer journal) for adults 18 and older, made by Amg Up.

The short version

Everything you write stays on your phone. FeLuz has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We cannot see your data, and we never sell or share it.

What FeLuz keeps (only on your phone)

In the private storage of the app, only what you write or choose:

  • Your intentions: the text, the notes, the type, your reminder choices, how many times you prayed for them and your thanks notes.
  • Your prayer journal: the days you prayed the rosary, the Chaplet, the Angelus or a day of a novena, and whom you offered it for.
  • Your novenas: the ones you start, your own novenas and prayers, and, for a novena for someone who has died, the name, the dates, the place and the anniversary you write, and the photo you choose.
  • Your rosary in progress, so you can pick it up where you left off.
  • Your reminders: the schedule that the app builds from what you turn on.
  • Your settings: language, text size, theme, the times of your reminders, the date your free trial started, and a few internal counters that decide when to show a card. The counters stay in the app and in your own backups; we never see them.

What FeLuz does not keep: your name, address, phone number, contacts, location, card numbers or any account. FeLuz never asks for them.

Sensitive information

Intentions can say what you believe and can talk about the health of other people. That is why they never leave your phone, except in these three ways, which you choose:

  • Android backup in your own Google account: only if your phone has a screen lock, encrypted by Google. Photos are not included in it: they travel only in your own backup file and when you move to a new phone with the direct phone-to-phone transfer of Android 12 or newer. We cannot read it, and you control it in your phone's settings.
  • The backup file (.zip) that you save wherever you want, and the spreadsheet files (CSV) that you export.
  • What you share by hand: an invitation, a card, a prayer, a printable novena (PDF, made on your phone), or the recommendation message. It goes to the app or person you choose, and the rules of that place apply from there.

Photos

You choose a photo with the Android photo picker, and FeLuz receives only that photo. It is reduced in size, stripped of its hidden data (such as location) and kept in the private storage of the app. It stays there until you remove it, use "Delete everything" or uninstall the app.

Reminders

Reminders are shown by your phone at the times you choose. A reminder never contains a name or the words of an intention, so nothing private appears on your lock screen. FeLuz does not use exact alarms, so a reminder can arrive a few minutes late.

Purchases

The subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are made through Google Play, under Google's own privacy policy. FeLuz only learns whether Pro is active. To make purchases work, the Google Play library connects to Google and may send technical data about the purchase and the phone; it never receives your intentions, your journal or your novenas.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for the reminders you turn on. FeLuz asks only when you turn one on.
  • Start at boot: to schedule your reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for the beads.
  • Read aloud (off until you turn it on): the words on the screen are read by the text-to-speech voice of your phone, the one chosen in Android settings. FeLuz sends nothing; that voice works under the rules of its maker.
  • Google Play purchases and internet: used only for the purchases and the age check described below.

FeLuz does not ask for location, contacts, microphone, camera, or access to your files or photos. When you rate FeLuz, the window is drawn by Google Play and sends nothing from FeLuz.

Deleting your data

You can delete an intention, a note or a novena in the app at any time. "Delete everything" in Settings (you type DELETE) removes all of it from your phone; it keeps only the date your free trial started, so that deleting does not restart the trial. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything from your phone. A backup file you saved yourself stays where you put it, and the Android backup is managed in your Google settings. Because we do not have your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Adults only

FeLuz is for adults 18 and older. It is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Age information from Google Play: in some places the law asks apps to take care with age. When you try to buy Pro, FeLuz may ask Google Play for your age range (for example, 18 and older). Google answers only with the range, how it was confirmed, or a note that a parent has not approved; FeLuz uses it at that moment to allow the purchase or to pause it, and does not save it, send it anywhere or link it to what you write. If Google does not answer, or does not share an age, nothing changes.

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom

Everything the app does with your data happens on your phone and is done by you; Amg Up receives nothing, so there is no processing by us, no legal basis to choose and no transfer outside your country. Your rights (access, correction, deletion, portability) you exercise directly in the app: export, back up, edit or delete; uninstalling removes everything except what Google Play keeps about your purchases under its own policy. If you have a question, write to the contact below.

Changes
