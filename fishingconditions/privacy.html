Fishing Conditions Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Leer en español

Who we are

Fishing Conditions is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means Amg Up. Privacy contact: pedalup180@gmail.com.

Short version

Fishing Conditions is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. It has no account, no servers for your data, no ads and no analytics. Your spots, your log, your photos and your settings stay on your phone.

Your location is optional and stays on your phone. To get the wind for a U.S. spot, the app sends a point rounded to about 6 miles (10 km) to the U.S. National Weather Service, never your exact spot. Tide times and feeding times are worked out on your phone.

A backup, CSV or PDF file, or a share card, leaves your phone only when you save or share it yourself. Payments are handled by Google Play.

What Fishing Conditions stores on your phone

You see the tide, feeding times, light and wind for your spots, keep a private log of your catches and learn fishing knots step by step. Fishing Conditions stores your spots, catches, outings, photos, notes and settings, on your phone. It lives in the private storage of the app. We do not receive it.

Location

Location is optional. If you tap "Use my location", Android asks you for your location (you can choose approximate). The app reads it once, while the app is open, and saves it on your phone as one of your spots. It never uses location in the background. You can also search a town or tide station, or paste coordinates, which works without the internet.

Internet and weather data

To show wind and pressure for a spot in the United States, Fishing Conditions sends a point rounded to 0.1 degrees (about 6 miles or 10 km) to the U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov), at most about once an hour while the app is open. It never sends your exact spot, your name, your log or anything you saved. You can turn off wind for any spot.

Like any website, that server can see the IP address of the connection. Tide times, feeding times, sun and moon times, patterns and knots all work on your phone without the internet. NOAA and the National Weather Service do not endorse this app.

Photos, alerts and sensors

Photos of your catches come from the camera or the photo picker of the system; the app keeps a reduced copy in its private storage and does not ask for camera or storage permissions.

Alerts are set up on your phone for what you turn on (the evening alert and the "still fishing?" alert), with the notifications permission. They do not go through any server. If your phone has a barometer, the app can read it while it is open; those readings stay on your phone.

What leaves your phone only when you choose

A backup file, a CSV or PDF of your log or tides, or a share card that you save or share with the Android menu. You pick where it goes. Share cards never include your coordinates or your notes, and the spot name is off unless you turn it on. "Open in Maps" hands the coordinates of a spot to the maps app you choose. The message you send when you recommend the app to a friend goes through the app you choose, not through us.

Android automatic backup

If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may copy the app files to your Google account, encrypted. This is a setting of your phone and Google, not of Fishing Conditions. It never includes purchases.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. Fishing Conditions only learns from Google Play whether Pro is active. The Google Play Billing library talks to Google for that; Fishing Conditions does not send your log or your places.

Age signals

When the app opens and before a purchase, the app may ask Google Play, through the Play Age Signals library from Google, about the age range of the account. The answer is used at that moment and is not stored or sent anywhere by Fishing Conditions.

Permissions

Location (optional, once, with the app open; never in the background), notifications (only for the alerts you turn on), start at boot (to put your alerts back after a restart), and internet and network state (wind from the U.S. National Weather Service, and purchases). Fishing Conditions does not ask for camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files permissions.

Children

Fishing Conditions is for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, and the app does not collect personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can view, export, back up and delete it from Settings, or by uninstalling the app. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to pedalup180@gmail.com with any question; since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new date appears at the top. Important changes are announced in the app or in Google Play.
