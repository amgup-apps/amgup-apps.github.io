3. Predictions, alerts and safety

Fishing Conditions is a companion for fishing. It is not professional advice and it is not a navigation tool.

(a) Tide times are predictions computed from NOAA data. They are not for navigation: real water levels change with wind, pressure, rivers and storms. (b) Wind is a forecast from the U.S. National Weather Service and can be wrong; check the official forecasts and warnings before you go out. (c) Feeding times are a traditional idea, not a promise. (d) Practice every knot and test it with a firm pull before you fish; the app does not promise how strong a knot is. (e) Know and follow the fishing rules, licenses and seasons where you fish; the app does not show them. (f) On the water, wear a life jacket, watch for lightning and respect private property. (g) Alerts depend on your phone and can arrive late or not at all.
