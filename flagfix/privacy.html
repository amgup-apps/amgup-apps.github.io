FlagFix Privacy Policy

Version 2, November 2026. FlagFix is a punch list and move-in / move-out report app for adults 18 and older, made by Amg Up.

The short version

Everything you type and every photo you take stays on your phone. FlagFix has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We cannot see your data, and we never sell or share it.

What FlagFix keeps (only on your phone)

In the private storage of the app, only what you type, take or choose:

  • Your business: name, phone, email, license number and logo, if you add them.
  • Your jobs: job name, address and type (punch list, make-ready, turnover, move-in / move-out), and the language of the report.
  • Your flags: the text or ready-made phrase, room, trade, who fixes it, due date, status, notes, photos with their date, and the arrows, circles and numbers you draw on a photo.
  • Other people's information (this is third-party data): clients, crew members, tenants and owners, with their names, phone numbers and the language they read. You are responsible for having permission to keep it.
  • Signatures: the picture of a finger-drawn signature, with the name and role you type.
  • Move-in / move-out records: the condition you mark for each item, notes, photos with their date and signatures.
  • Your own phrases, drafts, reminders, report log and settings.

Photos

You take photos with the camera of your phone or pick them with the Android photo picker. FlagFix does not ask for the camera or photos permission. Each photo is saved smaller in the private storage of the app, and its location and other metadata are removed. The original is never changed or deleted by FlagFix, except the temporary file the camera makes for it. The logo you choose in Settings is saved the same way, as a small separate file.

Voice dictation

The dictation button opens the voice service of your phone, which follows its own rules. FlagFix only receives the text. It does not ask for the microphone permission.

PDFs, backups and exports

FlagFix makes a PDF, a text message, a backup file (.zip), a CSV or a ZIP of photos only when you ask, and it goes where you choose (WhatsApp, email, Drive, Files, print). From there the policy of that service applies. The backup file is a plain .zip: it is not protected by a password, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Android backup

If you turned on Android backup, Android may save the data of the app (without the photos) in your own Google account, and may copy the data and the photos when you move to a new phone. We do not receive any of it.

Purchases

The purchase after the 30-day trial is handled by Google Play. FlagFix never sees your card. The app only learns whether it was purchased.

Reminders and permissions

Reminders for flags that are due are scheduled by your phone. FlagFix uses these permissions: notifications (to show them), restart (to schedule them again after the phone restarts), Google Play billing, and internet and network state (only for Google Play purchases; the screens of the app make no connections of their own). It does not use the camera, microphone, storage, photos, contacts, location or exact alarms.

Children

FlagFix is for adults 18 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the app; the Trash keeps deleted items for 30 days. Use "Delete all data" in Settings, or uninstall the app. Files you already sent or saved somewhere else stay there.

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom

Everything FlagFix does with your data happens on your phone, under your control. We do not receive, store or transfer your data, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To exercise your rights over your own data, use the app or uninstall it. Questions: pedalup180@gmail.com.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: pedalup180@gmail.com.
