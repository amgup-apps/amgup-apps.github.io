Health data notice

English · Español · Português · Français

This notice is the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy of Food Count. It is written for the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada Senate Bill 370 and similar laws of other states about consumer health data.

What health data Food Count handles. Only what you type or tap, in six groups. Food: the foods, amounts, meals, recipes and notes you log, the foods you plan for tomorrow, and the food choices you correct in Type a meal (what you typed and the food you picked). Weight: the weights you enter. Profile (optional): height, weight, year of birth, sex used for the formula, and activity level. Pregnancy or breastfeeding: only a yes or no, if you mark it. Fasting timer (optional): the start and end times of the fasts you record. Water counter (optional): the number of glasses you count each day.

Where it comes from. Only from you. Food Count does not read data from other apps, watches, scales or sensors. If you scan a barcode, the camera image is not saved and not shared: it stays inside the Google Play services scanner window, and Food Count only receives the number of the code, looks it up on your phone, and keeps nothing unless you add that food to your log.

Why. Food: to show your log, your totals and your trends. Weight: to show your weight trend. Profile: to give a starting estimate of calories and macros. Pregnancy or breastfeeding: to turn calorie targets and the fasting timer off. Year of birth, height and weight are also used to decide whether the fasting timer can be used. Fasting timer: to show your timer, your history and your chart. Water counter: to show your count and its chart. Nothing else.

Where it is kept. Only on your phone. Food Count has no account and no servers. If the backup of your phone is on, Android may keep an encrypted copy in your own Google backup; we cannot read it.

We do not collect, share or sell it. We receive none of it. We do not sell it or share it with anyone, and there are no ads, no analytics of ours and no profiling. If you use the barcode scanner, Google receives technical data about the scanner (device model, system version, app version, an installation identifier, performance and errors); none of your food, weight or profile data is part of it. A backup file, a CSV or a PDF leaves your phone only when you save or share it yourself, to the place you choose. Purchases go through Google Play, which never receives your log.

Your consent. Food Count saves your profile and your weight only when you enter them and tap the button that says they are saved only on this phone. Logging food works without a profile and without a weight. You can take your consent back at any time by deleting that data.

Your rights: see, export and delete. See and correct everything in the app. Export it with Settings > Back up and export (CSV, backup file or PDF). Delete one entry or one weight by opening it and tapping Delete. Change the profile in Settings > Plan and profile. Delete everything with Settings > Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to access or delete on our side, and using these rights never changes how the app works for you.

Estimates. Any estimate in the app comes from what you logged and is an estimate, not a measurement. Food Count sets no calorie targets during pregnancy or while breastfeeding, and keeps targets at or above a set minimum (1,200 or 1,500 calories a day).

Questions or complaints. Write to pedalup180@gmail.com. We answer within 45 days. If you live in Washington or Nevada and are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Attorney General of your state.

Food Count is not medical advice and not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Talk to a healthcare professional about your health.
