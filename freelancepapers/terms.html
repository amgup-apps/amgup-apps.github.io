Hours, pay, expenses, miles and jobs

Freelance Papers adds up what you type: hours, pay, expenses, miles, estimated profit and the status of your jobs. Everything is an estimate for your own use. It is not tax, payroll, accounting or legal advice: your pay stub, your tax preparer and the law of your state decide. The overtime multiplier and the weekly limit are numbers you write; Freelance Papers only multiplies them. The distance of a trip measured with GPS is an estimate that depends on the signal of your phone: check it and correct it before you save. Mileage values use rates that you can check and change in the app (source: the standard mileage rates published by the U.S. federal tax agency, checked November 14, 2026); the rate for a new year may not be in the app yet, and you are responsible for the rate you use. The dates for U.S. federal estimated tax payments are only a reminder: Freelance Papers does not calculate any amount, so check the date and what you owe with the federal tax agency or a tax preparer.

The acceptance record of a job or an estimate is a note kept on your phone, with the name your client types and the date and time. Freelance Papers only records what your client typed and tapped on your phone; it is not a service for signing documents electronically, and it does not check who the person is. Freelance Papers does not promise that the record has any legal effect. Keep your own signed agreement when your work needs one.
