Fridge Temps Privacy Policy

Version 4, November 20, 2026. Fridge Temps is a food safety log for kitchens: temperatures, cooling, receiving, sanitizer, checklists, food labels, cleaning and pests, with a PDF for your inspector. Made by Amg Up.

The short version

Fridge Temps does not collect your data and does not share it. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your records, photos, signatures and staff names stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

In the private storage of the app, Fridge Temps keeps only what you add or choose: your business name, address, permit number and phone (optional, for the header of your PDF), your equipment and areas, your limits, your staff names and initials (optional), your records (temperatures, notes, corrective actions, receiving, sanitizer, cooling, time as control, checklists, thermometer checks, food labels with their use-by dates, and cleaning and pest logs with the name of who did it if you write it), your day sign-offs, and your settings. Fridge Temps does not have a server and cannot read any of it.

Photos, signatures and your logo

Photos are optional. You take them with the camera app of your phone or pick them with the photo picker of Android. Fridge Temps shrinks each photo and saves it without any metadata, so without location data. A signature is a name and a drawn line that you make on the screen. Your logo is a picture you pick. All of them stay on your phone and are used only inside the app and in the PDF you make. Fridge Temps never analyzes them.

The internet

Fridge Temps uses the internet in only one case: for purchases and the state of your plan with Google Play. The pages of the app itself never use the network. Everything else, including logging, timers, History, the PDF and the CSV, works offline.

PDF, CSV, sharing and backups

Fridge Temps makes a PDF, a CSV, a daily summary, a backup (a file with your records and photos) or a message only when you ask. You then choose where it goes with the share sheet of your phone or the file picker of Android: a chat, an email, your files. From there the policy of that app or service applies. Fridge Temps does not send anything by itself.

Android backup

If you turn on Android automatic backup, Android may save your records and settings in your Google account, and only when the backup is encrypted. Photos, signatures and your logo are not included. When you move to a new phone with a cable or phone-to-phone transfer, the same data can move with you. This is a function of Android, controlled by you in the settings of your phone. Fridge Temps does not see it.

Reminders and notifications

Fridge Temps asks for the notifications permission for reminders to log a temperature, finish a cooling stage, check a thermometer or use a labeled food by its date, and the reminders 30 and 7 days before a yearly renewal. The reminders are set on your phone and are not exact alarms. They are turned off in the app, or in the settings of your phone, whenever you want. Fridge Temps also uses the vibration permission, so that saving a reading and the reminders can make your phone vibrate briefly; the vibration and silent settings of your phone always apply.

Rating and recommending

If you choose to rate Fridge Temps, the review window of Google Play opens and Fridge Temps never sees your review. Recommend opens the share sheet of your phone with a message and a link, and you choose who gets it.

Purchases

Purchases are made in Google Play, under its terms and privacy policy. Fridge Temps never sees your card. It only learns from Google Play which plan you have and its dates, and keeps that on your phone so it can unlock Pro.

Deleting your data

Settings has a Delete all data button. It erases your records, photos, signatures and settings from your phone. If you uninstall Fridge Temps, Android erases its storage too. A backup file or a PDF that you saved or shared stays where you put it.

What you decide about your staff

If you add the names of your staff, you decide what to keep and you tell them. Fridge Temps is a record-keeping tool and does not ask for health information about any person.

Who can use Fridge Temps

Fridge Temps is a business tool for adults, 18 or older. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them. When the app opens and before a purchase, Fridge Temps asks Google Play whether the account belongs to an adult (Play Age Signals); the answer is used at that moment, is not stored and does not leave your phone.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can view, export, back up and delete it in the app, or by uninstalling it. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to pedalup180@gmail.com with any question; since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Person in charge of privacy (Quebec) and contact for data subjects (Brazil, LGPD): Amg Up, pedalup180@gmail.com.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with its date. Questions: pedalup180@gmail.com.
