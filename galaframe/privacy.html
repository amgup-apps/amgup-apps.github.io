GalaFrame Privacy Policy

Version 1, October 2026. GalaFrame is a photo booth app for adults 18 and older who host a party or rent a booth, made by Amg Up.

The short version

GalaFrame has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers. It does not collect your data or send it anywhere.

The camera

The camera is used only while the booth is open on screen. GalaFrame does not use the microphone and never recognizes or analyzes faces.

GalaFrame asks for the camera permission. It also asks to save to the gallery on Android 9 or older, to use Google Play billing, and for internet and network state (only for Google Play purchases). It does not ask for the microphone, photos and videos, contacts, location, notifications or exact alarms permissions.

Photos, strips and GIFs

Photos, strips and GIFs stay on this device until you share, print, save them to the gallery or back them up. You choose the apps and the places for that. A backup file (.zip) holds your guests' photos, so keep it somewhere you trust.

In the private storage of the app, GalaFrame keeps only what you take, type or choose: your parties (name, date, theme and the texts of the frame), your strips, GIFs and photos, and your settings.

Printing, sharing and exporting

GalaFrame prints, shares, saves to the gallery or exports a ZIP only when you ask, and it goes where you choose. From there the policy of that app or service applies.

The booth PIN

The booth PIN stays on this phone and is never part of a backup.

Android backup

Android's automatic backup may copy your list of parties and your settings to your Google account. It does not copy the photos.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. The app only asks whether Pro is on.

Guests and minors

You are responsible for telling your guests that you take their photo, and for having a parent's permission to photograph minors, under the laws where you live.

GalaFrame is for adults 18 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

To delete everything, use Settings > Delete everything, or uninstall the app. Files you already shared or saved somewhere else stay there.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: pedalup180@gmail.com.
