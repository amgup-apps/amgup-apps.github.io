GalaFrame Privacy Policy

Version 3, November 9, 2026. GalaFrame is a photo booth app for adults 18 and older who host a party or rent a booth, made by Amg Up.

The short version

GalaFrame has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers. It does not collect your data or send it anywhere.

The camera

The camera is used only while the booth is open on screen. GalaFrame does not use the microphone and never recognizes or analyzes faces.

GalaFrame asks for the camera permission. It also asks to save to the gallery on Android 9 or older, to use Google Play billing, and for internet and network state (only for Google Play purchases). It does not ask for the microphone, photos and videos, contacts, location, notifications or exact alarms permissions.

Photos, strips and GIFs

Photos, strips and GIFs stay on this device until you share, print, save them to the gallery or back them up. You choose the apps and the places for that. A backup file (.zip) holds your guests' photos, so keep it somewhere you trust. If you turn it on, a guest can send their own strip to their phone with Quick Share, from the booth screen.

In the private storage of the app, GalaFrame keeps only what you take, type or choose: your parties (name, date, theme and the texts of the frame), your strips, GIFs and photos, and your settings.

Printing, sharing and exporting

GalaFrame prints, shares, saves to the gallery or exports a ZIP only when you ask, and it goes where you choose. From there the policy of that app or service applies.

If you say yes to "Copy strips to your phone gallery?" (it stays off until you do), each finished strip and GIF is also saved to the gallery of this phone. From there, other apps you use, such as a photo backup app, may upload it under their own rules, and Delete everything in GalaFrame does not remove those copies. You can turn it off in Settings.

If you turn on "Guests can send their strip to their phone", a guest can send their own strip and GIF from the booth screen with Quick Share, from your phone to theirs, nearby and without internet. Nothing else leaves your phone, and the strip also stays on it. It is off unless you turn it on.

The booth PIN

The booth PIN stays on this phone and is never part of a backup.

Android backup

Android's automatic backup may copy your list of parties and your settings to your Google account; it does not copy the photos. When you move to a new phone with Android's device transfer, the photos, GIFs and your own designs move with the app.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. The app only asks whether Pro is on.

Guests and minors

You are responsible for telling your guests that you take their photo, and for having a parent's permission to photograph minors, under the laws where you live.

GalaFrame is for adults 18 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

To delete everything, use Settings > Delete everything, or uninstall the app. Deleted strips wait 30 days in Recently deleted and are then erased for good. Copies already saved to your phone gallery stay there. Files you already shared or saved somewhere else stay there.

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom

Everything the app does with photos happens on your device and by your choice, so you are the one in charge of those photos (the household exemption usually applies to a private party; if you rent the booth, you are the controller for your clients). Amg Up receives no personal data from the app, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete; to erase your data, use Settings > Delete everything or uninstall the app. No data is transferred outside your device by us. If you have a question or a complaint, write to pedalup180@gmail.com; you can also contact your local data protection authority.

Accessibility

GalaFrame works with TalkBack, large text and "reduce motion". If something does not work for you with TalkBack or large text, write to pedalup180@gmail.com and we will fix it.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the version and the date at the top change too. Questions: pedalup180@gmail.com.
