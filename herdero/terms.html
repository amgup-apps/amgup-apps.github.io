What Herdero is

Herdero is a tool to keep your own cattle records on your phone and to make PDFs and files from them. Herdero keeps the records you type. It is not connected to any government system. It is not an official record, a health certificate or a bill of sale, and it does not give veterinary advice.

What you are responsible for

  • What you type, photograph and send, including every number, date and withdrawal time. Herdero does not check or calculate them.
  • Following the law and the rules of your place about animal identification, medicines, records and sales.
  • Having permission to keep other people's information (buyers, workers).
  • Asking your veterinarian about doses, withdrawal times and any animal health question.

Another phone

Sending a file to another phone and combining it there is not live sync. Each phone keeps its own copy. When both phones changed the same animal, the newer change is kept and the older details are saved in the history of that animal. A phone with another Google account has its own free days and its own purchase.

Your data and backups

Your data lives on your phone. We do not see it and we cannot recover it. Android may also save a limited backup in your Google account, but the backup file you make is the real backup. Save backup files where you choose.

Trial and purchase

Herdero is free for 30 days, with no card. After that, adding new records, working a group, importing a spreadsheet and combining another phone's file need one payment: US$39.99, one payment made through Google Play, which shows it in your currency, with no subscription. A group that is open when the trial ends can finish for up to 12 hours. Your records stay free to view, search, correct, print, export and back up. Refunds follow the rules of Google Play.
