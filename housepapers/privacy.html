House Papers Privacy Policy

Version 7, November 16, 2026. House Papers is a home inventory and home care app, for adults 18 and older, made by Amg Up.

The short version

Your list and your photos stay on your phone. House Papers has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We cannot see your data, and we never sell or share it.

What House Papers keeps (only on your phone)

In the private storage of the app, only what you type, photograph or choose:

  • Your inventory: your rooms, your items (name, category, quantity, brand, model, serial number, where and when you bought it, prices, condition and notes), your room walk-throughs and your claims.
  • Your photos: the photos of your items, labels, receipts and rooms, made smaller to save space. House Papers removes the location from every photo. Your original photos stay where they were.
  • Your home details: the name or address of your home, the people who live there, and your insurance company, agent and policy number if you choose to type them. This is sensitive information: it stays on your phone and in the files you export.
  • Your home care notes: the chores and seasonal jobs you switch on, your supplies, your service records (what was done, who did it, the date and the cost), warranty dates, your house plants (their names, rooms, watering and feeding days, notes and photos), your recipes, weekly menu and shopping lists, your tidy-up projects (name, room, notes and the before and after photos), your move-in and move-out reports (the date, the address or name of the place if you type it, the condition and notes of each room, and its photos), the expiry dates of your papers (never document numbers) and the optional "If something happens to me" sheet with the names, phone numbers and notes you type. They stay on your phone and in the files you share. House Papers never reads your contacts, and it asks you not to write passwords or account numbers.
  • Your settings: language, currency, paper size, reminders, the date your free trial started, plus a few internal counters that decide when to show a card. The counters never leave the phone.

What House Papers does not keep: your location, contacts, card numbers or any account. House Papers never asks for them.

When it leaves your phone

Only when you do it: when you save a backup (a ZIP file), make a PDF or a spreadsheet file (CSV), or send something with the share menu, it goes to the app or place you choose (for example a cloud drive, email or a message app), and the rules of that place apply from there. A backup is not encrypted: keep it in a private place. If Android backup is on in your phone, Android may keep a copy of your list and settings, without photos, in your own Google account; you control that in your phone's settings, and we cannot read it. If you move to a new phone and choose to copy your apps and data directly from the old one, Android may also move your photos from phone to phone.

Purchases

House Papers Pro (a subscription or a one-time purchase) is bought through Google Play, under Google's own privacy policy. House Papers only learns whether Pro is active; it never sees your card. To make purchases work, the Google Play library connects to Google and may send technical data about the purchase and the phone; it never receives your list or your photos. Pro comes only from Google Play, never from a backup file.

Permissions

Notifications: only for the reminders you turn on; they never show the names or the values of your things. Restart receiver: sets your reminders again when the phone restarts. Google Play purchases and internet: used only for prices and purchases. Photos come from the camera app or the photo picker of Android, and a backup is saved or opened with the file picker of Android, so House Papers needs no camera, photo, video or storage permission. House Papers does not use location or contacts.

Recommending the app

The button to recommend House Papers prepares a message with a link to the app. You choose whether to send it, and it never includes your list. The link to rate House Papers only opens a Google Play page.

If the app closes because of an error

If House Papers closes because of an error, it offers to share the error text with us. You decide whether to send it. That text has the app version, the Android version, the brand and model of the phone and the technical error; it never has your list, your home details or your photos.

Deleting your data

Use "Delete all data" in Settings (after a warning, it asks you to type DELETE), or uninstall the app, and everything is removed from your phone, except the date your free trial started, which stays so the trial cannot restart. Backups, PDFs and files you saved yourself stay where you put them; delete them there. Because we do not have your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Adults only

House Papers is for adults 18 and older. It is not directed to children under 13.

Age signals

When the app opens and before a purchase, the app may ask Google Play, through the official Play Age Signals library, whether the account belongs to an adult. The answer is used at that moment and is not stored or sent anywhere by House Papers.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can see it, export it, back it up and delete it there. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to us at the address below with any question; since we hold no personal data of yours, there is nothing we can look up, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with its date.
