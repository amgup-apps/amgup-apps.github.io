InchBoss Privacy Policy

Version 1, October 2026. InchBoss is a feet-inch-fraction calculator for the building trades, for adults 18 and older, made by Amg Up.

The short version

Everything you type stays on your phone. InchBoss has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We cannot see your data, and we never sell or share it.

What InchBoss keeps (only on your phone)

In the private storage of the app, only what you type or choose:

  • Your tapes: the calculations you make, with their results, names and notes.
  • Your cut lists: pieces, lengths, board sizes and the names you give them.
  • Your tool entries and drafts: the measurements you type into the job tools, and the trash.
  • Your settings: language, trade, rounding, paper size, sharing options and the date your free trial started, plus a few internal counters that decide when to show a card. The counters never leave the phone.

What InchBoss does not keep: your name, address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, card numbers or any account. InchBoss never asks for them.

When it leaves your phone

Only when you do it: when you send a tape or a cut list (for example by chat or text message), make a PDF, or save a backup file (.json) or a spreadsheet file (CSV), it goes to the app or place you choose, and the rules of that place apply from there. If Android backup is on in your phone, Android may keep a copy of InchBoss's data in your own Google account; you control that in your phone's settings, and we cannot read it.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is made through Google Play, under Google's own privacy policy. InchBoss only learns whether the purchase is active. To make purchases work, the Google Play library connects to Google and may send technical data about the purchase and the phone; it never receives your tapes or your cut lists.

Permissions

Google Play purchases and internet: used only for the purchase. Saving or restoring a backup uses the file picker of Android, so InchBoss needs no storage permission and reads only the file you pick. InchBoss does not use notifications, camera, contacts or location.

Other apps

The links to rate InchBoss or to see Kerchi (another app by the same developer) only open a Google Play page. They send no data from InchBoss.

Deleting your data

You can delete tapes and cut lists in the app at any time, and empty the Trash in Settings. Uninstalling the app deletes everything from your phone (a backup file you saved yourself stays where you put it). Because we do not have your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Adults only

InchBoss is a tool for work, for adults 18 and older. It is not meant for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with its date.
