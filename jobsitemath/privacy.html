Jobsite Math Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 (version 5). Jobsite Math is a feet-inch-fraction calculator for the building trades, for adults 18 and older, made by Amg Up.

The short version

Everything you type stays on your phone. Jobsite Math has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We cannot see your data, and we never sell or share it.

What Jobsite Math keeps (only on your phone)

In the private storage of the app, only what you type or choose:

  • Your tapes: the calculations you make, with their results, names and notes.
  • Your cut lists: pieces, lengths, board sizes, the names you give them, and the materials and prices you type for their cost.
  • Your tool entries and drafts: the measurements you type into the job tools, and the trash.
  • Your settings: language, trade, rounding, currency, paper size, sharing options and the date your free trial started, plus a few internal counters that decide when to show a card. The counters never leave the phone.

What Jobsite Math does not keep: your name, address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, card numbers or any account. Jobsite Math never asks for them.

When it leaves your phone

Only when you do it: when you send a tape or a cut list (for example by chat or text message), make a PDF, or save a backup file (.json) or a spreadsheet file (CSV), it goes to the app or place you choose, and the rules of that place apply from there. The backup file is plain text that anyone who has the file can read: keep it where you keep your work papers. If Android backup is on in your phone, Android may keep a copy of Jobsite Math's data in your own Google account; you control that in your phone's settings, and we cannot read it.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is made through Google Play, under Google's own privacy policy. Jobsite Math only learns whether the purchase is active. To make purchases work, the Google Play library connects to Google and may send technical data about the purchase and the phone; it never receives your tapes or your cut lists.

Age signals

When the app opens and before a purchase, the app may ask Google Play, through the official Play Age Signals library, whether the account belongs to an adult. The answer is used at that moment and is not stored or sent anywhere by Jobsite Math.

Permissions

Google Play purchases and internet: used only for the purchase. Saving or restoring a backup uses the file picker of Android, so Jobsite Math needs no storage permission and reads only the file you pick. Jobsite Math does not use notifications, camera, contacts or location.

Other apps

The links to rate Jobsite Math or to see another app by the same developer only open a Google Play page. They send no data from Jobsite Math.

Deleting your data

You can delete tapes and cut lists in the app at any time, and empty the Trash in Settings. Uninstalling the app deletes everything from your phone (a backup file you saved yourself stays where you put it). Because we do not have your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Adults only

Jobsite Math is a tool for work, for adults 18 and older. It is not meant for children.

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom

What you type is processed only on your phone, by you, for your own use. We do not receive it, so we do not transfer it anywhere, we do not profile you, and there is no legal basis to name because we process no personal data of yours. The only connection is the Google Play purchase, under Google's own policy. Your rights to see, correct, delete or move your data are in your own hands: everything is on your phone, and uninstalling the app deletes it all. There are no cookies or trackers. Questions or requests: pedalup180@gmail.com.
