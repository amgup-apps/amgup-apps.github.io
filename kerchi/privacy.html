Kerchi Privacy Policy

Version 2, November 2026. Kerchi is an invoice and estimate app for adults 18 and older, made by Amg Up.

The short version

Everything you type stays on your phone. Kerchi has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We cannot see your data, and we never sell or share it.

What Kerchi keeps (only on your phone)

In the private storage of the app, only what you type or choose:

  • Your business: name, logo, address, phone, email, website, license number and tax ID number, if you add them.
  • Your clients' information (this is other people's data): names, companies, phone numbers, emails, billing and job-site addresses, and your notes about them.
  • Your documents: invoices and estimates with their lines, prices, discounts, taxes, payments (date, amount, method and note), notes and payment instructions, the terms and conditions you choose or write, and the dates on which you shared each document or sent a reminder for it.
  • Your photos of the work: pictures you choose with the Android photo picker for an estimate or an invoice (up to 8 each). Kerchi saves a smaller copy in its private storage, without location data, and prints them on extra pages of the PDF only when you share it.
  • Your repeating invoices: which invoices you set to repeat (every week, every 2 weeks or every month) and the next date to bill. Kerchi sends nothing by itself: it only reminds you.
  • Your own services and prices ("My services"), drafts, the trash, the reminders and your settings (language, currency, tax, PDF options and the date your free trial started).

What Kerchi does not keep: card numbers, bank account numbers or Social Security numbers. Kerchi never asks for them. Please do not type them in notes.

Your clients' data

You are responsible for having permission to keep your clients' information and for using it only for your work with them. If a client asks you to delete their data, delete their documents and their client record in the app, and then empty the Trash in Settings.

When it leaves your phone

Only when you do it: when you send a PDF (with the photos of the work you added, if any) or a message, or save or share a backup file or a spreadsheet (CSV), it goes to the app or place you choose, and the rules of that place apply from there. If Android backup is on in your phone, Android may keep a copy of Kerchi's data in your own Google account; you control that in your phone's settings, and we cannot read it. Photos are not part of the automatic Android backup; they only travel in a phone-to-phone transfer or in the backup file you save.

Automatic backup (only if you turn it on in Settings): you pick one folder once, with the Android folder picker, and Kerchi saves a full backup file there by itself (kerchi-auto-DATE-TIME.zip: your clients, documents, payments, settings, logo and photos of the work). It does this when you open Kerchi or while you use it, only if something changed, and at most every 6 hours. It keeps the last 5 copies and deletes only its own older ones. Kerchi reads only the names of the files in that folder, to find its own older copies, and the file it has just written; it reads nothing else there. These files are not locked with a password: anyone who can open that folder can read them, and if the folder belongs to another app (Drive, OneDrive and others), the rules of that app apply. The folder stays on your phone or in the app you picked; we never receive it. You can turn this off in Settings; the files already saved stay in the folder until you delete them.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is made through Google Play, under Google's own privacy policy. Kerchi only learns whether the purchase is active. To make purchases work, the Google Play library connects to Google and may send technical data about the purchase and the phone; it never receives your clients or your documents. The rating window ("Rate on Google Play") is drawn by Google Play under the same policy; Kerchi receives nothing from it.

Permissions

Notifications: for the reminder on the day an invoice is due, or on the day an invoice you set to repeat is ready to review, only if you turn it on. Restart: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts. Google Play purchases and internet: used only for the purchase. Choosing a contact, a logo or photos of the work uses the pickers of Android, so Kerchi needs no permission for them and reads only what you pick.

Deleting your data

You can delete documents and clients in the app at any time, and empty the Trash. Uninstalling the app deletes everything from your phone (a backup file you saved yourself, and the automatic backup files in the folder you chose, stay where they are). Because we do not have your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Adults only

Kerchi is a tool for business, for adults 18 and older. It is not meant for children.

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom

Kerchi does not receive, store or process your personal data or your clients' data: everything stays on your phone, under your control, and what you do with it there is your own decision. We make no transfers and use no cookies or tracking. The only connection is the one Google Play makes for the purchase, under Google's terms. To delete your data, delete it in the app or uninstall the app. To ask anything or to complain, write to us at the address below or to your data protection authority.
