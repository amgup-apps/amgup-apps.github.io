Health data notice

Some of what you can enter in Macrito is health related: the foods you eat, your weight, and the optional profile (height, weight, year of birth, formula, activity and whether you are pregnant or breastfeeding).

We do not collect it. It is stored only on your phone. Macrito has no servers and sends nothing. We do not sell it, share it, use it for ads or use it to profile you.

You control it. You can edit or delete any entry, export everything (CSV, backup file, PDF) and erase it all with Settings > Delete all data. You can turn numbers off, or never enter a profile.

Estimates. Any estimate in the app comes from what you logged and is an estimate, not a measurement. Macrito sets no calorie targets during pregnancy or while breastfeeding, and keeps targets at or above a set minimum (1,200 or 1,500 calories a day).

Rights. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have a question, write to pedalup180@gmail.com.

Macrito is not medical advice and not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Talk to a healthcare professional about your health.
