Macrito: food and macro log

Macrito is a simple calorie and macro counter for Android. Log what you eat in seconds, in English or Spanish, with no account and no ads.

What it does. About 4,000 foods from USDA FoodData Central, including Latin dishes. Type in a nutrition label, build recipes, see trends, log your weight if you want, reminders you control, backup and export, and a PDF summary for your clinician or dietitian.

Private. What you log is stored on your phone. There are no servers, no analytics and no ads.

Food data. USDA FoodData Central (public domain). Macrito is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) or the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA).

Free trial. 30 days free, no card needed. After that, adding new entries needs Pro. Viewing, searching, exporting, backing up and deleting your log are always free.

For adults 18 and older. If food or weight feels hard right now, this may not be the right tool. Support is inside the app, in Settings.
