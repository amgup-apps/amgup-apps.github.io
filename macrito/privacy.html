Privacy policy

Who we are. Macrito is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means Amg Up. Privacy contact: pedalup180@gmail.com.

Short version. Macrito has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. What you log stays on your phone.

What Macrito stores on your phone. The foods you log, your weight if you enter it, the optional profile (height, weight, year of birth, formula, activity, whether you are pregnant or breastfeeding), your plan and settings, your own foods and recipes, and reminder settings. It lives in the private storage of the app. We do not receive it.

What leaves your phone only when you choose. A backup file, a CSV or a PDF that you save or share with the Android menu. You pick where it goes.

Android automatic backup. If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may copy the app files to your Google account, encrypted. This is a setting of your phone and Google, not of Macrito. It never includes the lock, purchases or an unfinished draft.

Purchases. Payments are handled by Google Play. Macrito only learns from Google Play whether Pro is active. The Google Play Billing library talks to Google for that; Macrito does not send your log.

Permissions. Notifications: for the reminders you turn on, and for two notices a few days before your 30 free days end (they say nothing about your food or weight). On Android 13 or newer, Macrito asks for this permission only when you turn reminders on. Restart: to set your reminder again after the phone restarts. Fingerprint: only if you turn on the lock; your phone checks the fingerprint, and Macrito never sees or stores it. Internet and network state: used only by the Google Play libraries for purchases and for the age check described below. Macrito does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or your files.

Support numbers. Find support opens your phone dialer with the number typed in. Macrito never calls by itself.

Food data. The food list comes from USDA FoodData Central and is inside the app. No internet is needed to search it. Some foods have a 1-serving size from the FDA reference amounts (21 CFR 101.12), a label reference and not a recommendation of how much to eat. Macrito is not affiliated with or endorsed by the USDA or the FDA.

Adults only. Macrito is for people 18 and older. If the year of birth you enter makes you younger than 18, Macrito saves only that the app is blocked, not your year.

Age information from Google Play. In some places the law asks apps to take care with age. When you try to buy Pro, Macrito may ask Google Play for your age range (for example, 18 and older). Google answers only with the range, how it was confirmed, or a note that a parent has not approved; Macrito uses it at that moment to allow the purchase or to pause it, and does not save it, send it anywhere or link it to your log. If Google does not answer, or does not share an age, nothing changes.

Deleting your data. Settings > Delete all data erases everything Macrito saved, except the date your free trial started, or uninstall the app. Nothing is kept on a server because nothing is sent to one.

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom. The person responsible for this app (the controller) is its developer, Amg Up, at pedalup180@gmail.com. Macrito does not collect or transfer personal data: your log stays on your phone, so you use your rights (access, correction, deletion and portability) directly in the app by exporting, editing or deleting. If you think your rights were not respected, you can complain to the data protection authority of your country.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
