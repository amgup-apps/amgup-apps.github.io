Moon Chart Privacy Policy

Version 4, November 16, 2026. Moon Chart is an astrology app (birth chart and moon calendar) for entertainment and reflection, for adults 18 and older, made by Amg Up.

The short version

Everything you type stays on your phone. Moon Chart has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We cannot see your data, and we never sell or share it. Moon Chart does not ask for your location: you type your birth place yourself.

What Moon Chart keeps (only on your phone)

In the private storage of the app, only what you type or choose:

  • Your birth data: your birth date, time and place, and the chart worked out from them.
  • The people you add: their names and birth data. Add only people who are OK with it. Their data stays on this phone.
  • Your saved days, with the notes you write, and the Trash.
  • Your alerts: the sky events you pick (full moon, new moon, Mercury retrograde) and the hour. They are scheduled on the phone.
  • Your settings: language, theme, motion, the date your free trial started, the date you last opened the app, plus a few internal counters that decide when to show a card. The counters never leave the phone.

What Moon Chart does not keep: your contacts, photos, location, card numbers or any account. Moon Chart never asks for them.

Places and time zones

The list of cities comes inside the app (GeoNames, CC BY 4.0) and the time zone rules come from your phone. Looking up a place does not use the internet.

When it leaves your phone

Only when you do it: when you share a picture of your chart or of the sky, make a PDF, or save a backup file (.json) or a spreadsheet file (CSV), it goes to the app or place you choose, and the rules of that place apply from there. We do not receive it. The backup file and the CSV are kept by you, wherever you choose to save them. If Android backup is on in your phone, Android may keep a copy of Moon Chart's data in your own Google account; you control that in your phone's settings, and we cannot read it.

Purchases

The yearly subscription and the one-time purchase are made through Google Play, under Google's own privacy policy. Moon Chart only learns whether the purchase is active. When the app opens and before a purchase, Moon Chart asks Google Play whether the account belongs to an adult (Play Age Signals); the answer is used at that moment, is not stored and does not leave your phone. To make purchases work, the Google Play library connects to Google and may send technical data about the purchase and the phone; it never receives your birth data or your people.

Permissions

Notifications: only for the sky alerts you turn on. Restart: to set your alerts again after the phone restarts. Vibration: for alerts and for button feedback. Google Play purchases, internet and the network state (a permission that comes with the Google Play library): used only for the purchase; the pages of the app never load anything from the internet. Saving or restoring a backup uses the file picker of Android, so Moon Chart needs no storage permission and reads only the file you pick. Moon Chart does not use location, contacts or camera.

Other apps

The link to rate Moon Chart only opens a Google Play page, and the share buttons use the sharing menu of Android. They send no data from Moon Chart on their own.

Deleting your data

You can delete each person and each saved day in the app (they go to the Trash, and you can empty the Trash in Settings). Uninstalling the app deletes everything from your phone (a backup file you saved yourself stays where you put it). Because we do not have your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Adults only

Moon Chart is for adults 18 and older. It is not meant for children.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can view, export, back up and delete it from the app, or by uninstalling it. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to pedalup180@gmail.com with any question; since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.
