NowKick Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026 · Leer en español

NowKick is a one task timer for Android: you pick one thing, start the timer, and NowKick asks "Did you finish?" when time is up. It is made by one person and published by Amg Up, the developer name shown on Google Play. This page explains what the app does with your data, in plain words.

The short version

  • NowKick does not collect, store on servers or share any data. Your things, times and settings stay on your phone, and in Android backup if you turned it on.
  • There is no account, no ads and no analytics. The app screens never connect to the internet; only Google Play does, for purchases.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. NowKick only learns whether Pro is active, never your payment details. The Google Play Billing library may send technical data to Google, under the Google Play terms.
  • NowKick does not collect consumer health data.

What NowKick stores on your phone

NowKick keeps these files in its private app folder, which other apps cannot read:

  • your things: the name you type (if you type one), the minutes, and when each timer started, paused and ended;
  • your history: what you finished, what you left as started and what ran out of time;
  • your settings (language, theme, sounds, vibration, the optional daily reminder, and whether the name shows in notifications);
  • the date your 30-day trial started and the last state of Pro that Google Play confirmed;
  • things you deleted, kept for 30 days in Recently deleted so you can restore them (then removed), and an unsent draft.

If you turn on Android backup, Android may copy these files (not the timer that is running) to your Google account. That copy is made by Android, under your Google account settings, not by NowKick.

You can also save your own backup file or export your history (CSV) with the system file picker, and keep them wherever you like. These files never leave your phone through NowKick.

Notifications, alarms and the lock screen

By default the name of the thing you are timing shows in the notification and on the lock screen. You can hide it in Settings. If you add the widget, names show on your home screen.

NowKick uses alarms and notifications only to ring when a timer ends and for the optional daily reminder. They are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere. Android asks your permission for notifications (Android 13 and newer).

Health data

Health data: NowKick never asks about your health and does not collect or infer health data. NowKick never receives the names of your things: they stay on your phone, in your Android backup if it is on, and in what you choose to share or export.

Consumer health data (Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada and similar laws): NowKick does not collect consumer health data.

Purchases (Pro)

NowKick has a 30-day trial inside the app (no card, it never renews by itself) and an optional Pro upgrade (monthly, yearly or lifetime) through Google Play Billing.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. NowKick only learns whether Pro is active, never your payment details. The Google Play Billing library may send technical data to Google, under the Google Play terms.

Internet

There is no account, no ads and no analytics. The app screens never connect to the internet; only Google Play does, for purchases.

Google Play also draws its own review window if you choose to use it; NowKick receives nothing from it.

Sharing

When you share your day or recommend NowKick, the image or the message goes only to the app and the person you choose. If the app closes because of an error, it can show a technical report that you may share with the developer; it has no names of your things.

Deleting your data

To delete your data, use Settings › Delete everything, clear the NowKick storage in the Android settings or uninstall NowKick. Backup files you saved stay where you put them.

Delete everything erases your things, history and settings from the phone and turns off the alarms. It does not cancel a subscription, remove a purchase or restart your free days: those belong to your Google account and to the trial date, and you manage purchases in Google Play.

Children

NowKick is meant for adults (see the Terms of use) and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and the "Last updated" date will change.
