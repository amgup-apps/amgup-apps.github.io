Using Page Aloud

Page Aloud reads text out loud for your personal use. You may only listen to, or save as audio, content you have the right to use. Page Aloud includes a small library of classic public-domain books; their sources and licenses are listed in Settings > Open-source licenses. Apart from those, Page Aloud does not sell or hand out books, articles or any content, and it is not a way to get around the rules of the people who made them. The classic books in Spanish, German, French and Portuguese come from Wikisource under the CC BY-SA 4.0 license (older contributions also under CC BY-SA 3.0 and GFDL). Our changed versions of those texts are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 too; that license covers those texts, not the app, and you can always export and share the text, with or without Pro.

What Pro unlocks

Pro lets you listen to your own documents and to the built-in classic books, add documents and save audio. You can add the classic books to your library and read them on screen without Pro, and you can listen to the first chapter of each book without Pro, but listening to the rest needs Pro. Without Pro, or when the 30 free days or a plan end, nothing is deleted: you can still see your library, read the text, search, use bookmarks and notes, export, back up, restore and delete. The sample document plays without Pro. If you cancel a paid period, you keep Pro until that period ends; nothing is charged after that.

Text extraction can have errors

Page Aloud works out the text of your files on your phone. Some files (scans, photos of pages, files with copy protection or odd layouts) cannot be read, or can be read with mistakes. We do not promise that Page Aloud reads every file well, or that the voice says every word correctly. The voices come from the text-to-speech engine of your phone.
