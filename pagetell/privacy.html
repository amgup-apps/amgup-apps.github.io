Pagetell Privacy Policy

Version 2, November 2026. Pagetell reads your PDFs, ebooks and articles out loud with the voices of your phone. Made by Amg Up.

The short version

Pagetell does not collect your data and does not share it. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your files and texts stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

In the private storage of the app, Pagetell keeps only what you add or choose: the documents you open (their text and covers), where you stopped, your bookmarks and notes, your list of pronunciations, how long you have listened, and your settings. Nothing is uploaded to a server, because Pagetell does not have one.

Your files

Pagetell opens a PDF, EPUB, TXT or HTML file only when you pick it, with the file picker of Android or with "Open with". It keeps a copy of the text inside the app and never changes the original file. If a PDF has a password, you type it to open the file and Pagetell does not save it.

The voice

Pagetell reads with the text-to-speech engine of your phone and chooses only voices that work without internet. Pagetell never sends your text to any server to read it. The engine on your phone belongs to its maker and follows its own policy. Pagetell never downloads voices by itself; Android installs them when you ask.

The internet

Pagetell uses the internet in only two cases:

  • When you ask for the text of a web link ("Get article text"): Pagetell downloads that one page straight from the site, which sees the address like a browser would. It goes to the site you chose, not to us. Only secure (https) pages are downloaded.
  • For purchases and the state of your plan with Google Play.

The site of the article can see that the request comes from Pagetell and the language of the app. If the app closes because of an error, it offers to share the text of the error (never your documents).

The pages of the app itself never use the network. Everything else, including reading, searching and saving audio, works without internet.

Saving audio, sharing, exporting and backups

Save as audio makes an M4A file on your phone (in the Music folder on Android 10 or newer, or where you choose). Pagetell shares a file, an export (TXT or CSV), a backup or a text only when you ask, and it goes where you choose. From there the policy of that app or service applies.

Android backup

If you turn on Android automatic backup, Android may save the list of your documents, your places, bookmarks and settings (not the texts) in your Google account, and only when the backup is encrypted. When you move to a new phone with a cable or phone-to-phone transfer, the texts can travel too. This is done by Android with your account, not by us.

Reminders and notifications

Pagetell asks for the notifications permission to remind you 2 days before your trial ends and 30 days and 7 days before a yearly renewal. The reminders are set on your phone and set again after a restart. If you tap "Add to my calendar", Pagetell opens your calendar app with the event filled in and does not read your calendar. While it reads with the screen off, a media notification shows pause, resume and next sentence.

Purchases

Purchases are made in Google Play, under its terms and privacy policy. Pagetell never sees your card. It only learns from Google Play which plan you have and its dates, and keeps that on your phone so it can unlock Pro.

Permissions

Pagetell asks for: internet (for the two cases above), Google Play billing and network state (for purchases), foreground service for media playback (to keep reading with the screen off), notifications, and start after a restart (to set the reminders again). It does not ask for storage, camera, microphone, contacts, location or exact alarms.

Children

Pagetell is for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children; we do not knowingly collect data from anyone. Plans are bought through Google Play under its rules.

Accessibility

Pagetell is built so more people can read and listen: Atkinson Hyperlegible or OpenDyslexic letters, big text, wide spacing, light, Paper and dark themes, and labels for TalkBack. These features run on your phone and send nothing anywhere.

Deleting your data

Delete a document in the app, and then remove it for good from Recently deleted, or uninstall Pagetell to remove everything it keeps. Files you already saved or shared somewhere else stay there.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: pedalup180@gmail.com.
