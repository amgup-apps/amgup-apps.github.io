Version 2, November 2026. Pagetell is made by Amg Up.

Using Pagetell

Pagetell reads text out loud for your personal use. You may only listen to, or save as audio, content you have the right to use. Pagetell includes a small library of classic public-domain books; their sources and licenses are listed in Settings > Open-source licenses. Apart from those, Pagetell does not sell or hand out books, articles or any content, and it is not a way to get around the rules of the people who made them. The Spanish classic books come from Wikisource under the CC BY-SA 4.0 license; that license covers those texts, not the app, and you can export the text.

Plans and prices

  • Yearly: 7-day free trial for new subscribers, then US$29.99 per year. Google Play asks for a payment method to start the trial and charges nothing for 7 days. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, Google Play charges the yearly price and renews it every year until you cancel.
  • Monthly: US$4.99 per month, without the 7-day trial, renews every month until you cancel.
  • Lifetime: US$59.99, a single payment. It is not a subscription and it never renews.
  • The price you see in the app comes from Google Play and can change by country, with taxes.

Cancel anytime in Google Play

Cancel in Pagetell Settings > Plan > Manage, which opens your subscriptions in Google Play, or in Google Play > Payments and subscriptions. Cancel before the trial ends to pay nothing. We remind you 2 days before your trial ends, and 30 days and 7 days before a yearly renewal, with a notification and the option to add it to your calendar; the reminder is a courtesy, so the date in Google Play is the one that counts. Refunds follow the policy of Google Play.

What Pro unlocks

Pro lets you listen to your own documents and to the built-in classic books, add documents and save audio. You can add the classic books to your library and read them on screen without Pro, and you can listen to the first chapter of each book without Pro, but listening to the rest needs Pro. Without Pro, or when a trial or plan ends, nothing is deleted: you can still see your library, read the text, search, use bookmarks and notes, export, back up, restore and delete. The sample document plays without Pro. If you cancel a paid period, you keep Pro until that period ends; nothing is charged after that.

Text extraction can have errors

Pagetell works out the text of your files on your phone. Some files (scans, photos of pages, files with copy protection or odd layouts) cannot be read, or can be read with mistakes. We do not promise that Pagetell reads every file well, or that the voice says every word correctly. The voices come from the text-to-speech engine of your phone.

Your data and backups

Your files and texts stay on your phone. You are in charge of keeping a backup (Settings > Backup and export) of what matters to you. If you lose your phone without a backup or without Android backup, we cannot recover it.
