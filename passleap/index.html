Pilot Prep helps you practice for the FAA Private Pilot and Sport Pilot airplane knowledge tests, with timed practice tests, a figure book, Flight Math, a METAR and TAF decoder and Listen mode, in English or Spanish. Apprentice Prep helps you practice for trades aptitude tests (electrical, line and elevator), with timed practice tests for each part, Math Bench, reading passages and mechanical drawings, in English or Spanish. It is an independent study app, not affiliated with or sponsored by any union, training program or test publisher. No account, no ads, and they work offline.
