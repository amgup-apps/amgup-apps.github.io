PassLeap is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government agency, including any state DMV. PassLeap EPA 608 helps you practice the refrigerant test by section: Core, Type I, II, III and Universal. It is not affiliated with the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) or any federal or state agency. PassLeap CDL helps you practice the 8 CDL knowledge tests and the pre-trip walk-around, in English or Spanish. It is not affiliated with the FMCSA, the U.S. Department of Transportation, or any state licensing agency. PassLeap Electrician helps you practice for journeyman electrician exams based on the NEC®, by edition (2020 and 2023), with Code Hunt, Calc Lab and a Tab plan, in English or Spanish. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA), any state or local licensing board, or any testing company. NEC® is a registered trademark of the NFPA. PassLeap Part 107 helps you practice for the FAA Remote Pilot (Part 107) knowledge test, with timed 60-question practice tests, a figure book with chart figures and a METAR and TAF decoder, in English or Spanish. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), the U.S. Department of Transportation, any federal or state agency, or any testing center. PassLeap Project Manager helps you practice for the PMP® exam, with 180-question mock exams, a mindset compass with 26 rules, a formula and chart lab and the facts to know before you apply, in English or Spanish. It is an independent study app, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Project Management Institute, Inc. (PMI). PMI, PMP, CAPM and PMBOK are registered marks of the Project Management Institute, Inc. PassLeap A&P helps you practice for the FAA Aviation Mechanic General (AMG) knowledge test, with timed 60-question practice tests, a figure book, Hangar Math and Listen mode, in English or Spanish. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), the U.S. Department of Transportation, any federal or state agency, any testing center, or any aviation maintenance school. PassLeap Pilot helps you practice for the FAA Private Pilot and Sport Pilot airplane knowledge tests, with timed practice tests, a figure book, Flight Math, a METAR and TAF decoder and Listen mode, in English or Spanish. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), the U.S. Department of Transportation, any federal or state agency, any testing center, or any flight school. PassLeap IT Support helps you practice for the CompTIA A+® Core 1 and Core 2 exams, with a Tech Bench of hands-on scenes, Number Drills and practice tests with a clock, in English or Spanish. It is an independent study app, not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by CompTIA, Inc. CompTIA and CompTIA A+ are trademarks or registered trademarks of CompTIA, Inc. PassLeap Apprentice helps you practice for trades aptitude tests (electrical, line and elevator), with timed practice tests for each part, Math Bench, reading passages and mechanical drawings, in English or Spanish. It is an independent study app, not affiliated with or sponsored by any union, training program or test publisher. No account, no ads, and they work offline.
