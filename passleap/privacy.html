PassLeap Privacy Policy

Version 5, November 9, 2026. This policy covers the PassLeap apps from Amg Up that work this way: PassLeap DMV, "PassLeap: DMV Permit Test 2026", PassLeap EPA 608, "PassLeap: EPA 608 Test Prep", PassLeap CDL, "PassLeap: CDL Test Prep", PassLeap Electrician, "PassLeap: Electrician Exam", PassLeap Part 107, "PassLeap: Part 107 Drone Test", PassLeap Project Manager, "PassLeap: Project Manager Exam", PassLeap A&P, "PassLeap: A&P Mechanic Test", PassLeap Pilot, "PassLeap: Private Pilot Test", and, as they come out, PassLeap IT Support, "PassLeap: IT Support Exam", PassLeap Apprentice, "PassLeap: Apprentice Aptitude". They all keep your data the same way.

The short version

PassLeap does not collect your data and does not share it. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your progress stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

In PassLeap A&P, PassLeap also keeps the written tests you mark in My A&P plan (the month you passed, or the day you failed), your Hangar Math scores, the zoom of each figure and your Listen mode settings. All of it stays on your phone.

In PassLeap Pilot, PassLeap also keeps the month you passed your written test (to show your checkride deadline), the Report codes you type, your Flight Math scores, the zoom of each figure and your Listen mode settings. All of it stays on your phone.

In PassLeap Apprentice, PassLeap also keeps your Math Bench scores and the topic you picked there, and the date of your last test if you type it (to show when you can retest). All of it stays on your phone.

In the private storage of the app, PassLeap keeps only what you do in it: your state and license (or, in PassLeap EPA 608, your certification goal, in PassLeap CDL, your CDL plan and the pre-trip checks you marked as said, and in PassLeap Electrician, the NEC edition you picked, your Code Hunt times, your Calc Lab score and the tabs you marked in your Tab plan, and in PassLeap Part 107, the month of your test or your last recurrent training and the zoom of each figure), your language and settings, your answers and the box each question is in, the questions you saved, your practice tests and their results, your study days and Ready score, a test date if you set one, the day you first opened the app (for the 3-day trial) and which plan you have. None of it is sent to us.

The questions

All practice questions, road sign and pre-trip walk-around drawings, the chart figures of PassLeap Part 107, and explanations are inside the app. The pages of the app do not load anything from the internet and they work without a connection. We write our own practice questions from public sources, such as each state's driver handbook, 40 CFR Part 82 and EPA guidance, or federal regulations (49 CFR) and state CDL manuals, or federal safety rules (OSHA, 29 CFR) and public pages from licensing boards, or 14 CFR Part 107, FAA handbooks and the FAA's public testing supplement (FAA-CT-8080-2H). PassLeap Project Manager, PassLeap IT Support and PassLeap Apprentice use questions we write from scratch; they do not copy text from PMI, CompTIA or any test publisher. PassLeap Electrician does not include the text of the NEC. Each answer shows its source and a link that opens in your browser only when you tap it.

The internet

The app uses the internet in only one case: Google Play, which handles your purchases, may talk to its servers. The links you tap (a source, your state's agency, epa.gov, faa.gov, these pages) open in your browser, and then that site follows its own policy.

The voice

If you tap Listen, PassLeap reads the question with the text-to-speech voice built into your phone. PassLeap never sends the text anywhere. In PassLeap CDL, Listen also reads the pre-trip phrases in English, and the "Practice saying it" mode does not record you: PassLeap does not use the microphone. The voice engine on your phone belongs to its maker and follows its own policy.

Reporting a problem, sharing and backups

Report a problem opens your email app with a draft that has the question number and the app version. Nothing is sent unless you press send. The "I passed" card, the recommend message and the backup file go through the share menu or the file picker of Android, only when you ask, and they go where you choose. On the PassLeap DMV card, your state is off unless you turn it on; no card ever carries a name.

Android backup

If you turn on Android automatic backup, Android may save a copy of your progress in your Google account, under your device settings and Google's terms. When you move to a new phone, your progress can come back that way. PassLeap does not see that copy.

Purchases

Purchases are made in Google Play, under its terms and privacy policy. PassLeap never sees your card. It only learns from Google Play whether you have a pass or Lifetime, and when a pass ends, and keeps that on your phone so it can unlock the app. The 3-day trial does not ask for a card or an account. The app may ask you once, with the review window of Google Play, to rate it.

Permissions

PassLeap asks for: Google Play billing (for purchases), and internet and network state (so Google Play billing can work). It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or notifications. It does not use an advertising ID.

In the United States, PassLeap may ask Google Play for your age range and whether a parent approved purchases, as some state laws require. It keeps only the answer on your phone and uses it to show or hide purchases.

The one exception is PassLeap A&P, whose Listen mode can keep reading with the screen off. It also asks for the foreground service permission (media playback) and, on Android 13 and later, for notifications, so Android can show Pause and Next while it reads. It starts only when you tap Start listening with the app open, stops about 10 minutes after you pause, collects no data, and if you say no to notifications it still reads.

PassLeap Pilot is the second exception: its Listen mode can also keep reading with the screen off, with the same foreground service permission (media playback) and, on Android 13 and later, the same notifications, so Android can show Pause and Next while it reads. It starts only when you tap Start listening with the app open, stops about 10 minutes after you pause, collects no data, and if you say no to notifications it still reads.

Children

PassLeap is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. PassLeap CDL, PassLeap Electrician and PassLeap Project Manager are for adults 18 and older. PassLeap DMV, PassLeap Part 107 and PassLeap IT Support are for people 13 and older. PassLeap EPA 608 is for people 16 and older. Plans are bought through Google Play under its rules.

PassLeap A&P is for people 16 and older; the mechanic certificate itself requires being at least 18.

PassLeap Pilot is for people 13 and older. You can take the written test from about age 15, and the pilot certificate itself requires age 17 (student pilot certificate, 16).

PassLeap Apprentice is for people 16 and older.

Deleting your data

In Settings, "Delete my progress" erases what the app keeps and also the copies of it that the app made for Android backup. Uninstalling the app removes everything it keeps on your phone. A backup file you saved somewhere else stays there until you delete it.
