PassLeap A&P Terms of Use

Version 1, November 2026. These terms are for the app PassLeap A&P ("PassLeap: A&P Mechanic Test"), made by Amg Up ("we").

What PassLeap is

PassLeap is a study aid: practice questions for the FAA Aviation Mechanic General (AMG) knowledge test, the first written test for the mechanic certificate with airframe and powerplant ratings (often called an A&P license), with timed 60-question practice tests in the format of the test, a figure book with figures from the FAA's public testing supplement, Hangar Math (endless shop math with the steps), Listen mode (your phone reads the questions aloud with its own voice), a planner for the 24 months of 14 CFR 65.71 and a readiness score. The General test is the only test in the app for now. It is for your own use. PassLeap A&P is for people 16 and older; the mechanic certificate itself requires being at least 18.

PassLeap is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), the U.S. Department of Transportation, any federal or state agency, any testing center, or any aviation maintenance school. We write our own practice questions from public sources, such as 14 CFR Parts 43 and 65 and FAA handbooks. The figures come from the FAA's public testing supplement (FAA-CT-8080-4G). For official information, visit faa.gov.

This app does not give you a certificate

Only the FAA issues the mechanic certificate, after you pass the knowledge, oral and practical tests. The format, the cost and the rules of the real tests can be different from the practice here.

This app is not maintenance data

This app is not maintenance data. Never use PassLeap to maintain, inspect or return an aircraft to service: use the manufacturer's data and FAA-accepted data.

The figures are test figures

The figures are test figures: never use them on an aircraft. Three of them are drawings made by PassLeap, and they say so.

Rules change

Rules change: check the date shown in the app and faa.gov. Our questions can have mistakes. The problems in Hangar Math are made up for practice, with numbers that are not data of a real aircraft.

Listen mode

Listen mode reads with the voice that is already on your phone and can keep going with the screen off. While it reads, Android shows a notification with Pause and Next. You decide when it starts and you can stop it at any time. Use it only where it is safe to listen.

No promise about your test

PassLeap does not promise that you will pass. Practice tests and the readiness score are estimates from your own practice. My A&P plan counts months and days from the dates you enter; your test report and your examiner have the exact dates.

The 3 free days

The trial lasts 3 days from the first time you open the app. It does not ask for a card or an account. When it ends you need a plan to take timed practice tests, study every topic, use Hangar Math and Listen mode, and use today's study and mistake review again. The figure book and My A&P plan stay open, and your progress, your settings and your backups stay available.
