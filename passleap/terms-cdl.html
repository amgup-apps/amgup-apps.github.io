Version 3, November 16, 2026. These terms are for the app Trucker Prep ("Trucker Prep: CDL Permit Test"), made by Amg Up ("we"). Trucker Prep is one of the PassLeap study apps.

What Trucker Prep is

Trucker Prep is a study aid: practice questions for the 8 CDL knowledge tests, practice tests in English with Spanish on request, a pre-trip walk-around you can listen to and say out loud, and a readiness ring for each test, to help you prepare for your state's commercial driver's license (CDL) tests. It is for your own use, and only for adults 18 and older.

Trucker Prep is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the FMCSA, the U.S. Department of Transportation, or any state licensing agency, and it is not affiliated with AAMVA. We write our own practice questions in our own words from federal rules (49 CFR). Study your state's official CDL manual too. This app is not entry-level driver training (ELDT) and does not count toward it. For official information, visit your state's driver licensing agency: https://www.usa.gov/state-motor-vehicle-services

This app does not license you

Only your state licensing agency can give you a commercial learner's permit or a CDL, and it sets its own tests, fees and rules. Some states use a different test format, and your real test may be in English only. The "Before your test" list only tells you what to check with your state; it is not a list of legal requirements.

Rules change

Federal and state rules change. Check your state's CDL manual and your state licensing agency before you rely on a rule. Our questions can have mistakes.

No promise about your test

Trucker Prep does not promise that you will pass. Practice tests and the readiness ring are estimates from your own practice. The pre-trip walk-around is a study aid: your examiner's list can be different.

The 3 free days

The trial lasts 3 days from the first time you open the app. It does not ask for a card or an account. When it ends you need a plan to take practice tests and study again. The General Knowledge practice test stays open without a plan, and you can repeat it. The pre-trip walk-around never needs a plan, and your progress, your settings and your backups stay available.
