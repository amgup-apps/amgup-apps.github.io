PassLeap CDL Terms of Use

Version 1, November 2026. These terms are for the app PassLeap CDL ("PassLeap: CDL Test Prep"), made by Amg Up ("we").

What PassLeap is

PassLeap is a study aid: practice questions for the 8 CDL knowledge tests, practice tests in English with Spanish on request, a pre-trip walk-around you can listen to and say out loud, and a readiness ring for each test, to help you prepare for your state's commercial driver's license (CDL) tests. It is for your own use, and only for adults 18 and older.

PassLeap is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the FMCSA, the U.S. Department of Transportation, or any state licensing agency. We write our own practice questions from public sources, such as federal regulations (49 CFR) and state CDL manuals. This app is not entry-level driver training (ELDT) and does not count toward it. For official information, visit your state's driver licensing agency: https://www.usa.gov/state-motor-vehicle-services

This app does not license you

Only your state licensing agency can give you a commercial learner's permit or a CDL, and it sets its own tests, fees and rules. Some states use a different test format, and your real test may be in English only. The "Before your test" list only tells you what to check with your state; it is not a list of legal requirements.

Rules change

Federal and state rules change. Check your state's CDL manual and your state licensing agency before you rely on a rule. Our questions can have mistakes.

No promise about your test

PassLeap does not promise that you will pass. Practice tests and the readiness ring are estimates from your own practice. The pre-trip walk-around is a study aid: your examiner's list can be different.

The 3 free days

The trial lasts 3 days from the first time you open the app. It does not ask for a card or an account. When it ends you need a plan to take practice tests and study again. The pre-trip walk-around never needs a plan, and your progress, your settings and your backups stay available.

Your progress stays on your phone (see the Privacy Policy). You are in charge of your backups: save a backup file from Settings whenever you want. If you delete your progress or uninstall the app, we cannot get it back.
