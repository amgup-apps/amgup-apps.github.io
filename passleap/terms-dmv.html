PassLeap DMV Terms of Use

Version 1, November 2026. These terms are for the app PassLeap DMV ("PassLeap: DMV Permit Test 2026"), made by Amg Up ("we").

What PassLeap is

PassLeap is a study aid: practice questions, road signs, practice tests and a Ready score to help you prepare for your state's permit test, for a car or a motorcycle. It is for your own use.

PassLeap is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government agency, including any state DMV. We write our own practice questions from public sources, such as each state's driver handbook. For official information, visit your state's driver licensing agency: https://www.usa.gov/state-motor-vehicle-services

No promise about your test

PassLeap does not promise that you will pass. Practice tests and the Ready score are estimates from your own practice. Our questions can have mistakes and rules change, so your state's driver handbook is what counts. Where the app says it does not have your state's details, you practice with national questions.

The 3 free days

The trial lasts 3 days from the first time you open the app. It does not ask for a card or an account. When it ends you need a plan to study and take practice tests again. Your progress, your saved questions, your settings and your backups stay available.
