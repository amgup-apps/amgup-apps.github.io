PassLeap Electrician Terms of Use

Version 1, November 2026. These terms are for the app PassLeap Electrician ("PassLeap: Electrician Exam"), made by Amg Up ("we").

What PassLeap is

PassLeap is a study aid: practice questions for journeyman electrician exams based on the NEC®, by edition (2020 and 2023), with Code Hunt (a timer to find answers in your own code book), Calc Lab (endless new calculations with the steps), a Tab plan and a readiness score, to help you prepare for your state's journeyman electrician exam. It is for your own use, and only for adults 18 and older.

PassLeap is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA), any state or local licensing board, or any testing company. NEC® and National Electrical Code® are registered trademarks of the NFPA. We write our own practice questions from public sources and do not include the text of the NEC. Bring the code book your candidate bulletin allows. This app is for exam practice only, not for real installations.

This app does not license you

Only your state or local licensing board can give you an electrician license, and it sets its own exam, fees and rules. The format of your real exam (number of questions, time, parts, language, which code book and edition, and whether tabs and a calculator are allowed) can be different from the practice format here. The "Before your test" list only tells you what to check with your board; it is not a list of legal requirements.

Not an installation guide

PassLeap is for exam practice only. It is not a guide for real installations, designs or inspections. The code book you are allowed to bring, your local rules and your inspector are the ones that rule. Rules change with each edition, and our questions can have mistakes.

No continuing education

PassLeap does not give continuing education hours or credits.

No promise about your test

PassLeap does not promise that you will pass. Practice tests, Code Hunt times and the readiness score are estimates from your own practice.

The 3 free days

The trial lasts 3 days from the first time you open the app. It does not ask for a card or an account. When it ends you need a plan to take practice tests, Code Hunt, Calc Lab and Break 10 again. The Tab plan and the "Before your test" list stay open, and your progress, your settings and your backups stay available.
