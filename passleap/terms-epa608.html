What Refrigerant Prep is

Refrigerant Prep is a study aid: practice questions, practice exams by section, a vacuum-table trainer, a rule timeline and a readiness ring for each section, to help you prepare for the EPA Section 608 refrigerant test. It is for your own use.

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to these terms and approve any purchase in Google Play. If Google Play tells the app that the account belongs to a minor who does not have that approval, the app does not offer purchases; the trial days still work.

Refrigerant Prep is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) or any federal or state agency, and it is not a certifying organization. We write our own practice questions from public sources, such as 40 CFR Part 82 and EPA guidance. For official information and the list of certifying organizations, visit https://www.epa.gov/section608

This app does not certify you

Only a certifying organization on the EPA list can give you the Section 608 certification. This app is for practice. Car A/C (Section 609) is a different certification and is not covered.

Rules change

Refrigerant rules change. Rules that change show the date we checked them ("Rule checked"): check that date and epa.gov/section608 before you rely on a rule. Our questions can have mistakes. Your certifying organization decides how its test is given and scored.

No promise about your test

Refrigerant Prep does not promise that you will pass. Practice exams and the readiness ring are estimates from your own practice.

The 3 free days

The trial lasts 3 days from the first time you open the app. It does not ask for a card or an account. When it ends you need a plan to take practice exams and study again. One full practice test for the license or test you picked stays open without a plan, and you can repeat it. The vacuum trainer and the rule timeline never need a plan, and your progress, your settings and your backups stay available.
