PassLeap EPA 608 Terms of Use

Version 1, November 2026. These terms are for the app PassLeap EPA 608 ("PassLeap: EPA 608 Test Prep"), made by Amg Up ("we").

What PassLeap is

PassLeap is a study aid: practice questions, practice exams by section, a vacuum-table trainer, a rule timeline and a readiness ring for each section, to help you prepare for the EPA Section 608 refrigerant test. It is for your own use.

PassLeap is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) or any federal or state agency, and it is not a certifying organization. We write our own practice questions from public sources, such as 40 CFR Part 82 and EPA guidance. For official information and the list of certifying organizations, visit https://www.epa.gov/section608

This app does not certify you

Only a certifying organization on the EPA list can give you the Section 608 certification. This app is for practice. Car A/C (Section 609) is a different certification and is not covered.

Rules change

Refrigerant rules change. Rules that change show the date we checked them ("Rule checked"): check that date and epa.gov/section608 before you rely on a rule. Our questions can have mistakes. Your certifying organization decides how its test is given and scored.

No promise about your test

PassLeap does not promise that you will pass. Practice exams and the readiness ring are estimates from your own practice.

The 3 free days

The trial lasts 3 days from the first time you open the app. It does not ask for a card or an account. When it ends you need a plan to take practice exams and study again. The vacuum trainer and the rule timeline never need a plan, and your progress, your settings and your backups stay available.
