What PassLeap is

PassLeap is a study aid: practice questions for the CompTIA A+® Core 1 (220-1201) and Core 2 (220-1202) exams, with a Tech Bench of 12 hands-on scenes, Number Drills, practice tests of 90 questions with a clock, and a readiness score for each exam. It is for your own use. PassLeap IT Support is for ages 13 and up.

PassLeap is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by CompTIA, Inc. CompTIA and CompTIA A+ are trademarks or registered trademarks of CompTIA, Inc. We write our own practice questions and do not include text from CompTIA materials. PassLeap does not sell exam vouchers.

This app does not give you a certification

Only CompTIA issues the certification. Its rules for the exams, the languages, the fees and the retakes are the ones that count: check comptia.org before you book. PassLeap does not give you a score from CompTIA. The format, the cost and the rules of the real exams can be different from the practice here.

The practice goal

The goal shown in the practice tests (72 of 90, which is 80% correct) is only a practice goal set by PassLeap. It is not a CompTIA score. To learn how the real exams are scored, see comptia.org.

Rules change

The exams change: check the date shown in the app and comptia.org. Our questions can have mistakes. The tickets, names and numbers in the questions and scenes are made up for practice.

No promise about your exam

PassLeap does not promise that you will pass. Practice tests and the readiness score are estimates from your own practice.

The 3 free days

The trial lasts 3 days from the first time you open the app. It does not ask for a card or an account. When it ends you need a plan to take practice tests, study every topic and use the Tech Bench. Your mistakes (to read), "Before you book", your progress, your settings and your backups stay available.
