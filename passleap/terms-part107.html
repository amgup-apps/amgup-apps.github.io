What Drone Drill is

Drone Drill is a study aid: practice questions for the FAA Remote Pilot knowledge test (Unmanned Aircraft General - Small, from 14 CFR Part 107), with timed 60-question practice tests in the format of the test, a figure book with chart figures from the FAA's public testing supplement, a METAR and TAF decoder with endless practice, the date of your recurrent training and a readiness score. It is for your own use. Drone Drill is for people 13 and older; the Remote Pilot Certificate itself requires being at least 16 years old.

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to these terms and approve any purchase in Google Play. If Google Play tells the app that the account belongs to a minor who does not have that approval, the app does not offer purchases; the trial days still work.

Drone Drill is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), the U.S. Department of Transportation, any federal or state agency, or any testing center. We write our own practice questions from public sources, such as 14 CFR Part 107 and FAA handbooks. The figures come from the FAA's public testing supplement (FAA-CT-8080-2H). For official information, visit faa.gov/uas.

This app does not give you a certificate or permission to fly

Only the FAA issues the Remote Pilot Certificate, after you pass the knowledge test at an FAA-approved testing center. Drone Drill does not give flight authorizations, live maps or airspace alerts, and it is not a tool for planning a flight. The format, the cost and the rules of the real test can be different from the practice here.

The figures are old test figures

The figures in the app are old test figures: never use them for navigation. Three of them are drawings made by Drone Drill, and they say so.

Rules change

Rules change: check the date shown in the app and faa.gov. Our questions can have mistakes. The METAR and TAF reports in the decoder are made up for practice and never show the weather of a real place or day.

No promise about your test

Drone Drill does not promise that you will pass. Practice tests and the readiness score are estimates from your own practice.

The 3 free days

The trial lasts 3 days from the first time you open the app. It does not ask for a card or an account. When it ends you need a plan to take timed practice tests, study every topic and use today's study and mistake review again. One full practice test for the license or test you picked stays open without a plan, and you can repeat it. The figure book and the METAR and TAF decoder (with its practice) stay open, and your progress, your settings and your backups stay available.
