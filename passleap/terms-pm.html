What PassLeap is

PassLeap is a study aid: practice questions for the PMP® certification exam, with 180-question mock exams in 240 minutes with breaks, a mindset compass with 26 rules, a formula and chart lab, the facts to know before you apply, and a readiness score. It is for your own use. PassLeap Project Manager is for adults 18 and older.

PassLeap is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Project Management Institute, Inc. (PMI). PMI, PMP, CAPM and PMBOK are registered marks of the Project Management Institute, Inc. We write our own practice questions and do not include text from the PMBOK® Guide or from PMI's Examination Content Outline. This app does not count toward the 35 training hours or toward keeping a certification.

This app does not give you a certification

Only the PMI issues the certification. Its rules for who can apply, the application and the exam are the ones that count: check pmi.org. PassLeap does not give training hours and does not count toward keeping a certification. The format, the cost and the rules of the real exam can be different from the practice here.

The 70% is a practice goal

The 70% shown in the mock exams and practice tests is only a practice goal set by PassLeap. It is not a passing score from the PMI. To learn how the real exam is scored, see pmi.org.

Rules change

The exam and the rules of the PMI change: check the date shown in the app and pmi.org. Our questions can have mistakes. The cases, names and numbers in the questions are made up for practice.

No promise about your exam

PassLeap does not promise that you will pass. Mock exams and the readiness score are estimates from your own practice.

The 3 free days

The trial lasts 3 days from the first time you open the app. It does not ask for a card or an account. When it ends you need a plan to take mock exams and practice tests, study every topic, practice a rule and solve the formula lab's problems. The 26 mindset rules and the "Before you apply" facts stay open, and your progress, your settings and your backups stay available.
