What Project Prep is

Project Prep is a study aid: practice questions for the PMP® certification exam, with 180-question mock exams in 240 minutes with breaks, a mindset compass with 26 rules, a formula and chart lab, the facts to know before you apply, and a readiness score. It is for your own use. Project Prep is for adults 18 and older.

The 70% is a practice goal

The 70% shown in the mock exams and practice tests is only a practice goal set by Project Prep. It is not a passing score from the PMI. To learn how the real exam is scored, see pmi.org.

Rules change

The exam and the rules of the PMI change: check the date shown in the app and pmi.org. Our questions can have mistakes. The cases, names and numbers in the questions are made up for practice.

Project Prep does not promise that you will pass. Mock exams and the readiness score are estimates from your own practice.

The 3 free days

The trial lasts 3 days from the first time you open the app. It does not ask for a card or an account. When it ends you need a plan to take mock exams and practice tests, study every topic, practice a rule and solve the formula lab's problems. One Quick 10 a day stays open without a plan. The 26 mindset rules and the "Before you apply" facts stay open, and your progress, your settings and your backups stay available.
