Terms of Use

Pet Papers is a folder for your dog or cat: vet visits, vaccines, medicines, weight, symptom notes, food, walks, expenses and documents, with reminders, plus dog training with a built-in clicker. No account, no ads, and your pets and their records stay on your phone. Pet Papers keeps your notes; it does not give veterinary advice.
