Pet Papers Privacy Policy

Version 3, November 18, 2026. Pet Papers is a folder for your dog or cat: care records, reminders and dog training with a clicker. Made by Amg Up.

The short version

Pet Papers does not collect your data and does not share it. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your pets, their records, photos, documents and progress stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

In the private storage of the app, Pet Papers keeps only what you add or choose: your pets (name, dog or cat, age group, look, optional photo, date of birth, sex, breed, microchip, license and insurance numbers, and the phone numbers of your vet and emergency clinic), your care records (vet visits and their cost, vaccines, medicines and preventives, weights, symptom notes, documents, care reminders, the bathroom log and the litter box log), the checks you make on the Today tab, your training goals, house words, plan and progress for each skill, your training sessions and notes, your reminder choices and your settings (language, currency, weight unit and clock). Nothing is uploaded to a server, because Pet Papers does not have one.

Not veterinary advice

Pet Papers keeps your notes and reminds you of what you wrote. It does not give veterinary advice, does not calculate doses and does not decide vaccine dates. It never reads, scores or analyzes your records.

Photos and PDF files

A photo of your pet is optional, one per pet. A record (a vaccine, a vet visit, a symptom note or a document) can carry one photo or one PDF. You pick them with the photo picker or the file picker of Android. Pet Papers shrinks each photo, saves it without location data (EXIF) and never analyzes it; a PDF is copied as it is. They stay on your phone unless you share a card that includes your pet's photo, save a backup, or open a PDF: in that case Pet Papers hands a read-only copy to the PDF app you choose on your phone.

Region, currency and units

The first time you open it, Pet Papers suggests your currency, weight unit and clock from the language, time zone and clock format of your phone. It does not use your location, and the suggestion never leaves your phone. You can change it in Settings.

The clicker

The clicker plays its own sounds and can vibrate. It listens to the volume buttons or a Bluetooth clicker only while the app is open, and keeps the screen on only during a session. Pet Papers does not use the microphone or the camera.

The internet

Pet Papers uses the internet in only one case: for purchases and the state of your plan with Google Play. The pages of the app itself never use the network. Everything else, including your records, reminders, the plan, the lessons, the clicker and your progress, works without internet. The Terms and Privacy links open in your browser.

Sharing, exporting and backups

Pet Papers shares a card (an image), a file, an export (CSV), a backup (a ZIP with your records, photos and PDF files) or a text message to WhatsApp or another app only when you ask, and it goes where you choose. From there the policy of that app or service applies. Backups and exports are saved where you pick with the file picker of Android, and restoring reads only the file you pick. A backup is not encrypted by Pet Papers: keep it in a place you trust.

Android backup

If you turn on Android automatic backup, Android may save your pets, care records, their photos and PDF files, progress, sessions and settings in your Google account, and only when the backup is encrypted. When you move to a new phone with a cable or phone-to-phone transfer, the same things can travel. This is done by Android with your account, not by us.

Reminders and notifications

Pet Papers asks for the notifications permission the first time you save a record with a reminder. It uses notifications for the care reminders you create (medicines and preventives, vaccine dates, vet appointments, documents that expire and care tasks), the daily training reminder (with an optional second one), the potty reminder that tells you when it may be time to go out (off until you turn it on) and the reminders 30 and 7 days before a yearly renewal. A care reminder shows your pet's name and the name you wrote, never the dose; on a locked phone that hides sensitive content it shows only "A pet care reminder". The reminders are set on your phone and set again after a restart or a change of time or time zone. They are not exact alarms, so they can arrive a few minutes late.

Rating and recommending

If you choose to rate Pet Papers, the review window of Google Play opens and Pet Papers never sees your review. Recommend opens the share sheet of your phone with a message and a link, and you choose who gets it.

Purchases

Purchases are made in Google Play, under its terms and privacy policy. Pet Papers never sees your card. It only learns from Google Play which plan you have and its dates, and keeps that on your phone so it can unlock Pro.

Permissions

Pet Papers asks for: internet and network state and Google Play billing (for purchases), notifications, start after a restart (to set the reminders again) and vibration (for the clicker). It does not ask for storage, camera, microphone, contacts, location or exact alarms.

If the app closes unexpectedly

Pet Papers can show an error screen with a Share button. The error text goes nowhere unless you tap Share, and you choose where it goes.

Children

Pet Papers is for adults, 18 or older, and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Children can train a dog together with an adult. Plans are bought through Google Play under its rules. Before a purchase, Pet Papers asks Google Play whether the account belongs to an adult (Play Age Signals); the answer is used at that moment, is not stored and does not leave your phone.

Deleting your data

In Settings, Delete all data erases your pets, records, photos, documents, progress and sessions from your phone. You can also delete one pet, one record or one session (with Undo for a few seconds), or uninstall Pet Papers to remove everything it keeps. Files you already saved or shared somewhere else stay there.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can view, export, back up and delete it in the app, or by uninstalling it. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to pedalup180@gmail.com with any question; since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: pedalup180@gmail.com.
