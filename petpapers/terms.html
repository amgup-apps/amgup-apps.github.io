Using Pet Papers

Pet Papers is a place to keep your own notes about your dog or cat (vet visits, vaccines, medicines, weights, symptoms and documents), to be reminded of what you wrote, and to learn dog training with rewards. It does not replace a veterinarian or a professional dog trainer. The safety of your pet, of you and of other people is your responsibility. For aggression, strong fear or panic when alone, work with a professional dog trainer or a veterinary behaviorist. Children train with an adult.

Not veterinary advice

Pet Papers does not give veterinary advice. It does not calculate doses, does not decide which vaccines your pet needs or when, does not suggest a weight or a food amount, and does not tell you what a symptom means. The dates, doses and amounts in the app are the ones you write, from your vet or your certificate. If you are worried, call your vet. In an emergency, call your vet or an emergency clinic now.

Reminders can be late

Reminders are a courtesy. They are not exact alarms: Android can delay or silence them (battery saving, Do Not Disturb, notifications turned off). Do not rely on a reminder alone for something that must not be missed; the Today tab always shows what is due.

Your records are your copy

The photos and PDF files you keep in Pet Papers are your own copy. The record that counts stays with your vet, your county or your insurer. Pet Papers does not issue certificates.

No promise of results

Every dog learns at its own pace. Pet Papers does not promise any result or any time frame.

Plans and prices

Cancel anytime in Google Play

Cancel in Pet Papers Settings > Plan > Manage, which opens your subscriptions in Google Play, or in Google Play > Payments and subscriptions. We remind you 30 and 7 days before a yearly renewal, with a notification; the reminder is a courtesy, so the date in Google Play is the one that counts. Refunds are handled by Google Play, under its rules and the law that applies where you live (for example, the right of withdrawal in the European Union and the United Kingdom).

Pro unlocks adding new records (vet visits, vaccines, medicines and preventives, weights, symptom notes, documents, care reminders, foods and meal times, walks, grooming reminders and expenses) and new reminders, the litter box log, the daily training plan, the 14-day plans, the potty log, the lessons (except the 6 basic ones: Name game, Marker, Sit, Down, Touch and Come), new training sessions, the guides (except the potty guide), the training reminders and more than one pet.
