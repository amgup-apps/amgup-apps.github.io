Photo Estimates Privacy Policy

Version 8, November 18, 2026. Photo Estimates is an estimate and invoice app for adults 18 and older, made by Amg Up.

The short version

Everything you type stays on your phone, with one exception that you control: when you tap "Make draft", the photos and the note for that estimate go to our AI server to make a draft, and the server keeps none of it. Photo Estimates has no account, no ads and no analytics. We never sell your data and never use it for ads.

What Photo Estimates keeps (only on your phone)

In the private storage of the app, only what you type or choose:

  • Your business: name, logo, address, phone, email, website, license number and tax ID number, if you add them.
  • Your clients' information (this is other people's data): names, companies, phone numbers, emails, billing and job-site addresses, and your notes about them.
  • Your documents: invoices and estimates with their lines, prices, discounts, taxes, payments (date, amount, method and note), notes and payment instructions, the terms and conditions you choose or write, and the dates on which you shared each document or sent a reminder for it.
  • Your photos of the work: pictures you choose with the Android photo picker for an estimate or an invoice (up to 8 each), for an expense receipt (1) or for a note about a client (1). Photo Estimates saves a smaller copy in its private storage, without location data, and prints the photos of the work on extra pages of the PDF only when you share it.
  • Your price list: your items with their unit and price, your overhead and profit percentages, and your trade.
  • Your answers to the welcome questions (language, currency, feet or meters, trade, overhead and profit, and your hourly rate), so the app can set itself up. They are part of your settings and of your backup.
  • AI drafts: for an estimate made from photos, the estimate keeps the draft number, the name of the AI model, a short summary, the warnings and questions, your note and whether you checked every line. None of this is printed on the PDF.
  • A random installation number, made by the app for the count of AI drafts. It is not your phone number or the ID of your phone.
  • Your hours, shifts and expenses: the time you log with the clock or by hand (date, minutes, client and task), the shifts in "My pay" (start and end times, break, tips, and the pay rate, overtime multiplier and weekly limit you type), your expenses (amount, date, category, client and note) with the photo of the receipt if you add one, the expenses you set to repeat, and the hourly rates you type. Photo Estimates only adds up what you type. All of it stays on your phone.
  • Your trips (miles or kilometers): the trips you write yourself or measure with GPS (date, from, to, purpose, type, vehicle, distance and odometer numbers, and whether the trip was written by hand or measured with GPS), your saved places and vehicles (names you type), and the table of rates by date.
  • Location, only for a trip with GPS that you start: when you tap "Start trip with GPS", Photo Estimates reads the precise location of this phone to add up the distance, and it stops when you tap "Finish". It keeps measuring with the screen off or with another app open, and a fixed notification shows the whole time. Photo Estimates keeps only the total distance and the start and end time of the trip. It does not save the route or any coordinates, it never reads your location when no trip is running, it never asks for location "all the time", and the trip is never sent anywhere. If you never start a trip with GPS, Photo Estimates never asks for location.
  • Your jobs and notes: the jobs you follow (client, title, price options, deposit and stage), the acceptance record (the name the person types and the date and time, kept on this phone only), and your notes about each client, with a photo if you add one.
  • Your repeating invoices: which invoices you set to repeat (every week, every 2 weeks or every month) and the next date to bill. Photo Estimates sends nothing by itself: it only reminds you.
  • Your own services and prices ("My services"), drafts, the trash, the reminders and your settings (language, currency, tax, PDF options and the date your free trial started).
  • Files you choose to bring in: if you import clients or services from a CSV file (or restore a backup), Photo Estimates reads the file with the Android file picker only to show you a preview and to add what you confirm. It does not keep the file. The clients and services you add are stored like the rest of your data.

What Photo Estimates does not keep: card numbers, bank account numbers or Social Security numbers. Photo Estimates never asks for them. Please do not type them in notes.

Your clients' data

You are responsible for having permission to keep your clients' information and for using it only for your work with them. If a client asks you to delete their data, delete their documents and their client record in the app, and then empty the Trash in Settings.

AI drafts from photos (only when you tap "Make draft")

Only when you tap "Make draft", or answer the questions of a draft, Photo Estimates sends to our AI server: 1 to 4 photos you choose, made smaller (1,280 pixels on the long side) and saved again, so they carry no location, date, phone model or other hidden data; your note, with emails, phone numbers and long numbers changed to [removed]; the measurements you type; your list of items without prices (only their name, unit and number); your trade, the app language, the estimate language and feet or meters; the random installation number; and, if you have Pro, the Google Play purchase token, so the server can check your plan.

Never sent: your client's name, phone, email or address; your business name, logo, license or other details; your prices, overhead, profit or totals; your other estimates and invoices; your location; voice recordings.

Voice: if you use the microphone button, the speech service of your phone turns your voice into text, under its own rules, and Photo Estimates only receives the text. Photo Estimates does not record audio and does not ask for the microphone permission.

Who handles it: our AI server runs on Cloudflare, Inc. (United States), which receives the request and sees your IP address when your phone connects. The server passes the photos and the text to Anthropic, PBC (United States), whose AI model makes the draft. Both act on our behalf and only to make your draft. To check a plan, the server asks Google Play.

What is kept: our server keeps none of the photos, the note or the draft. It keeps only counters: how many AI drafts an installation number used during the trial, or a plan used this month (a plan is kept as a fingerprint of the purchase token, never the token itself), a mark that a purchase was checked (6 hours), the total cost of the month, and, if you tap Report, the draft number and your reason (90 days). Anthropic may keep what it receives for up to 30 days to check for misuse, and it does not use it to train its models. The draft that comes back is saved only on your phone.

Without signal: the request waits on your phone (up to 10) and goes out when you open the app with signal or tap "Send now". The waiting request does not carry your purchase token. In Settings, "AI and privacy" shows what leaves your phone and has "Delete my queued photos".

Photos of a home: you are responsible for having permission to take and send photos of the place you work on. Do not include faces, addresses or license plates in the photos you send. The draft is a help: it can be wrong, the prices are always your own, and you check every line before you send anything to a client.

When it leaves your phone

Only when you do it: when you tap "Make draft" (see above), when you send a PDF (with the photos of the work you added, if any) or a message, or when you save or share a backup file or a spreadsheet (CSV). A PDF, message or file goes to the app or place you choose, and the rules of that place apply from there. If Android backup is on in your phone, Android may keep a copy of the Photo Estimates data in your own Google account; you control that in your phone's settings, and we cannot read it. Photos are not part of the automatic Android backup; they only travel in a phone-to-phone transfer or in the backup file you save.

Automatic backup (only if you turn it on in Settings): you pick one folder once, with the Android folder picker, and Photo Estimates saves a full backup file there by itself (photo-estimates-auto-DATE-TIME.zip: your clients, documents, payments, settings, logo, photos of the work, price list, and your hours, shifts, expenses, receipts, trips, jobs and notes). It does this when you open Photo Estimates or while you use it, only if something changed, and at most every 6 hours. It keeps the last 5 copies and deletes only its own older ones. Photo Estimates reads only the names of the files in that folder, to find its own older copies, and the file it has just written; it reads nothing else there. These files are not locked with a password: anyone who can open that folder can read them, and if the folder belongs to another app (Drive, OneDrive and others), the rules of that app apply. The folder stays on your phone or in the app you picked; we never receive it. You can turn this off in Settings; the files already saved stay in the folder until you delete them.

Subscription and purchases

Photo Estimates Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription, or Lifetime, a one-time purchase, made through Google Play under Google's own privacy policy. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in Google Play. Photo Estimates only learns which plan is active. To make purchases work, the Google Play library connects to Google and may send technical data about the purchase and the phone; it never receives your clients or your documents. When you have Pro and tap "Make draft", the purchase token goes to our AI server, which asks Google Play if the plan is active (see above). The rating window ("Rate on Google Play") is drawn by Google Play under its own policy; Photo Estimates receives nothing from it.

Permissions

Notifications: for the reminder on the day an invoice is due, or on the day an invoice you set to repeat is ready to review, only if you turn it on. Restart: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts. Google Play purchases and internet: used for the purchase and, only when you tap Make draft, for the AI draft. Location (precise and approximate, only while you use the app) and the foreground service for location: only for a trip with GPS that you start and finish yourself; Photo Estimates asks for it the first time you start one, never in the background, and you can refuse and write your trips by hand. Choosing a contact, a logo, photos of the work or for a draft, a receipt or a note photo uses the pickers of Android, so Photo Estimates needs no permission for them and reads only what you pick. There is no camera or microphone permission.

Deleting your data

You can delete documents and clients in the app at any time, and empty the Trash. To delete everything at once, use "Delete all data" in Settings, or uninstall the app. Either one deletes everything from your phone, including the AI requests waiting for signal (a backup file you saved yourself, and the automatic backup files in the folder you chose, stay where they are). Our AI server keeps none of your photos, notes or drafts, so there is nothing of your content to delete there; its counters hold no content. To ask us anything about it, write to the address below.

Adults only

Photo Estimates is a tool for business, for adults 18 and older. It is not meant for children.

Age signals

When the app opens, before a purchase and before an AI draft, the app may ask Google Play, through the official Play Age Signals library, whether the account belongs to an adult. AI drafts are only for adults. The answer is used at that moment and is not stored or sent anywhere by Photo Estimates.

Where Photo Estimates is offered

This version is not offered in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or Japan. Wherever you are, you can ask us what we hold about you or ask us to delete it, at the address below; because our server keeps no content, the answer is usually that there is nothing to delete. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
