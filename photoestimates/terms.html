AI drafts

When you make an estimate from photos, an AI model makes a draft of the work and the quantities. The draft is a help for you to start from. It is not a quote, an offer or advice from Amg Up, and it can be wrong: it can miss work, misread a photo or guess a quantity. Amg Up does not set prices, does not quote your clients and does not visit the job. The prices always come from your own price list, with your overhead and profit.

You check every line, change what is needed and decide what you send. Lines without a price or a quantity stop the PDF until you fix or delete them, and before the first PDF of an AI draft you confirm "I reviewed every line". The PDF is yours: it carries your business, your license and your clauses, and it does not say that AI helped.

The warnings in a draft (mold, lead paint, asbestos, electrical, structure, roof safety, permits) inform, they are not advice: check them yourself and follow the rules of your trade and your state.

You need permission to photograph the place you work on, and you must not include faces, addresses or license plates in the photos you send. Use Report in a draft to tell us that it was wrong, unsafe or showed private information.

AI drafts have a count: 5 AI drafts during the free trial, 60 a month with a monthly or yearly subscription, and 30 a month with Lifetime. AI drafts may rest for a while when the service is busy or the budget of the month runs out; your estimate by hand always works, and a draft that fails does not use your count.

Hours, pay, expenses, miles and jobs

Photo Estimates adds up what you type: hours, pay, expenses, miles, estimated profit and the status of your jobs. Everything is an estimate for your own use. It is not tax, payroll, accounting or legal advice: your pay stub, your tax preparer and the law of your state decide. The overtime multiplier and the weekly limit are numbers you write; Photo Estimates only multiplies them. The distance of a trip measured with GPS is an estimate that depends on the signal of your phone: check it and correct it before you save. Mileage values use rates that you can check and change in the app (source: the standard mileage rates published by the U.S. federal tax agency, checked November 14, 2026); the rate for a new year may not be in the app yet, and you are responsible for the rate you use. The dates for U.S. federal estimated tax payments are only a reminder: Photo Estimates does not calculate any amount, so check the date and what you owe with the federal tax agency or a tax preparer.

The acceptance record is a note kept on your phone, with the name your client types and the date and time. It is not a signed document, and Photo Estimates does not promise that it has any legal effect. Keep your own signed agreement when your work needs one.
