PilotProof Privacy Policy

Version 2, November 2026. PilotProof is a pilot logbook for adults 18 and older, made by Amg Up. What changed since version 1: the automatic backup to a folder you choose, the grade and certificate number of a pilot named in an endorsement, the paragraph for the European Union and the accessibility line.

The short version

Everything you type stays on your phone. PilotProof has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We cannot see your data, and we never sell or share it.

What PilotProof keeps (only on your phone)

In the private storage of the app, only what you type, import or choose:

  • Your details, if you give them: your name and certificate number (printed on the PDF pages) and your certificate level.
  • Your dates: the date and class of your medical exam, whether you were 40 or older on the exam day (PilotProof never asks for your date of birth), BasicMed dates, your flight review, IPC and other checks, and your own dates. PilotProof never asks for or keeps a health condition.
  • Your flights: dates, airports, aircraft, times, landings, approaches, notes, and the extra columns of a file you import. Also your aircraft: tail numbers, types and classes.
  • Data of other people: the names of instructors, students and safety pilots that you type and, when an instructor signs, the signature drawn on your screen with the instructor's name and certificate number. Ask the instructor before you save a signature. If they ask you to remove it, delete it in the app (Signature, Remove signature) and then empty the Trash.
  • Endorsements you create (with the name and, for some endorsements, the grade and certificate number of the pilot named in them), your import history, drafts, the Trash and the schedule of your reminders.
  • Your settings: language, hours format, PDF options, the date your free trial started, plus a few internal counters that decide when to show a card. The counters never leave the phone.

What PilotProof does not keep: your date of birth, address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, card numbers or any account. PilotProof never asks for them.

When it leaves your phone

Only when you do it: when you share a PDF, a CSV file, a backup file (.zip), a milestone picture or a message, it goes to the app or place you choose, and the rules of that place apply from there. If Android backup is on in your phone, Android may keep a copy of PilotProof's data in your own Google account; you control that in your phone's settings, and we cannot read it.

Automatic backup: if you choose a folder in More, Your data, PilotProof writes a backup file (.zip) in that folder after each saved flight, signature or import, and keeps the last 5. Only you, and the apps you gave that folder to, can read those files; if the folder belongs to another app, that app's rules apply from there. Nobody else receives them. You can turn it off at any time in the same place.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is made through Google Play, under Google's own privacy policy. PilotProof only learns whether the purchase is active. To make purchases work, the Google Play library connects to Google and may send technical data about the purchase and the phone; it never receives your flights, dates or signatures.

Permissions

Notifications: to remind you of your dates, on your phone. If a reminder does not arrive, the reminders screen can explain why: for that, PilotProof reads some phone settings (battery restriction, Do Not Disturb, the reminder category) only on your phone, and sends nothing anywhere. Restart: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts. Google Play purchases and internet: used only for the purchase. Saving, restoring or importing a file uses the file picker of Android, so PilotProof needs no storage permission and reads only the file you pick. PilotProof does not use the camera, contacts or location.

Other apps

The link to rate PilotProof only opens a Google Play page. It sends no data from PilotProof.

Deleting your data

You can delete flights, signatures and endorsements in the app at any time, and empty the Trash. Uninstalling the app deletes everything from your phone (a backup or PDF file that you saved yourself stays where you put it). Because we do not have your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Adults only

PilotProof is a tool for pilots, for adults 18 and older. It is not meant for children under 13.

If you are in the European Union

PilotProof does not process your data on any server: everything happens on your phone, under your control, so there is no profiling and no transfer to other countries. To delete your data, uninstall the app or delete it inside the app. For any request about your data, write to the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with its date.
