The app as it is

PilotProof is given "as is". We work to make it useful and careful, but we cannot promise it will never have a mistake.

You are responsible for your logbook

Under 14 CFR 61.51, your logbook is yours to keep. You answer for what you log, for the signatures you ask for and for checking your currency dates. The dates PilotProof shows come from what you logged and from how the app reads the rules on the day of this version. Rules change, so check them.

What PilotProof is not

PilotProof does not replace your instructor, your aviation medical examiner, your examiner, your company rules or the FAA. It is an independent app, with no link to the FAA or any other government agency. Night time and cross-country time are entered by you; the app does not work them out.

Electronic signatures

The instructor signature on the screen is a record kept on your phone, with a mark if the flight changes afterwards. Whoever reviews your logbook (an examiner, an inspector, an employer) decides whether to accept it. If you are asked for paper, print the pages and sign in ink.

Importing

Check what you import. PilotProof shows what did not match, but it cannot know whether the file it came from was right.

Purchase and free trial

You have 30 days free from the first time you open the app, with no card and no automatic charge. After that, adding new flights, importing and creating new endorsements need a one-time purchase made through Google Play; it stays on your Google Play account and refunds follow the rules of Google Play. Your logbook, your dates, reminders, PDF, CSV and backup stay free.

Your files

Your data and your backup files are yours. Keep your own backups; PilotProof keeps your data only on your phone.

Changes

If these terms change, the new version will be on this page with its date.
