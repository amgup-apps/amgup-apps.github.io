Quiltkeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Leer en español

Who we are

Quiltkeep is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means Amg Up. Privacy contact: pedalup180@gmail.com.

Short version

Quiltkeep is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. It has no account, no servers for your data, no ads and no analytics. What you save stays on your phone.

A backup or CSV file leaves your phone only when you save or share it yourself. Payments are handled by Google Play.

What Quiltkeep stores on your phone

You calculate how much fabric you need, design a quilt on a grid of traditional blocks, keep your fabric stash with photos and follow each project step by step.: the fabrics you add (name, color, type, yards, store and price if you type them, up to three photos each), the projects you create (design, sizes, fabrics, steps with dates, photos and notes), the calculations you save, an optional logo for the PDF, your sewing settings, units, theme and language, and the date you first opened the app. It lives in the private storage of the app. We do not receive it.

What leaves your phone only when you choose

A PDF, a text list (shopping list or cut list), a CSV of your fabric stash or a backup .zip file (with your photos and logo) that you save or share with the Android menu. You pick where it goes. The photos you attach are copied, reduced and stripped of location data into the private folder of the app; they only leave your phone inside a PDF or a backup that you share. The message you send when you recommend the app to a friend goes through the app you choose (for example WhatsApp), not through us.

Android automatic backup

If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may copy the app files to your Google account, encrypted. This is a setting of your phone and Google, not of Quiltkeep. It never includes purchases, your photos or an unfinished draft.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. Quiltkeep only learns from Google Play whether Pro is active. The Google Play Billing library talks to Google for that; Quiltkeep does not send your projects or photos.

Age signals

Before a purchase the app may ask Google Play, through the official Play Age Signals library, whether the account belongs to an adult. The answer is used at that moment and is not stored or sent anywhere by Quiltkeep.

Permissions

Internet and network state: used by the Google Play libraries for purchases. Quiltkeep does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or access to your files: photos and the logo come in through the Android picker, which gives the app only what you choose, and the app does not take pictures.

Adults only

Quiltkeep is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can view, export, back up and delete it from Settings, or by uninstalling the app. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to pedalup180@gmail.com with any question; since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new date appears at the top. Important changes are announced in the app or in Google Play.

Contact

Questions about privacy: pedalup180@gmail.com
