Quizloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Leer en español

Who we are

Quizloom is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means Amg Up. Privacy contact: pedalup180@gmail.com.

Short version

Quizloom is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it does not keep your notes on a server. Your decks, progress and settings stay on your phone.

A backup or CSV file leaves your phone only when you save or share it yourself. Payments are handled by Google Play.

AI generation is the one exception: when you tap Generate or Explain more, the notes you chose (text, photos or PDF pages) are sent to our server (hosted by Cloudflare, Inc.) and to Anthropic to make your deck or the explanation. We do not store them, but Anthropic may keep them for up to 30 days for safety and, as far as it tells us, does not use them to train its models. The app also sends a random installation identifier and, with Pro, the Google Play proof of purchase (token), only so the limit works. Upload only notes you wrote or have permission to use.

What Quizloom stores on your phone

You add your notes (pasted text, photos or PDF pages), get flashcards and questions, and study them offline. Quizloom stores your decks (cards and questions), your study progress, your exam results, the reports you sent, the trash, your theme and language, and the date you first opened the app. It lives in the private storage of the app. We do not receive it.

What leaves your phone only when you choose

A backup file or a CSV file that you save or share with the Android menu. You pick where it goes. The message you send when you recommend the app to a friend goes through the app you choose (for example WhatsApp), not through us.

Android automatic backup

If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may copy the app files to your Google account, encrypted. This is a setting of your phone and Google, not of Quizloom. It never includes purchases or an unfinished draft.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. When you use AI generation with Pro, the app sends the Google Play proof of purchase to our server, which checks it with Google to confirm that Pro is active and keeps only a short-lived check of that proof. Quizloom never receives your card or payment details.

Age signals

Before a purchase the app may ask Google Play, through the official Play Age Signals library, whether the account belongs to an adult. The answer is used at that moment and is not stored or sent anywhere by Quizloom.

Permissions

Internet and network state: used by the Google Play libraries for purchases and by AI generation. Quizloom does not ask for permission to use your camera, location, contacts, microphone or storage: photos come from the Android camera app or the photo picker, and PDFs from the Android file picker, only when you choose them.

AI generation (artificial intelligence)

When you generate a deck or ask for an explanation, the data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to a server we operate on Cloudflare, Inc. (United States), which forwards it to Anthropic (United States), the AI provider. What is sent: the text you pasted, or the photos or PDF pages you chose (as images), the options you picked (type, number, language), an installation identifier and, if you have Pro, the Google Play proof of purchase. Photos and pages are reduced on your phone first, and nothing is sent until you tap the button. Like any internet request, the server and the AI provider see the IP address of your connection while they answer; we do not store it. Cloudflare may keep technical logs of requests (date, route, status) for a short time under its own policy.

Our server does not store your notes, photos, pages, questions or answers. It keeps only: a count of AI generations (per installation for the free ones, per month for Pro), a short-lived check that your purchase is valid, a daily total for all users, and the reports you send (the identifier of the generation and your reason). Anthropic processes the content under its own privacy and API terms; as far as it tells us, it does not use API content to train its models and may keep it for a limited time (up to 30 days) for safety.

The installation identifier is a random code that the app creates by itself the first time. It is not your device identifier or your account, and it is used only to count the free generations. Uninstalling the app creates a new one.

Do not include data about other people, such as names, phone numbers, addresses or grades, in what you upload. AI results can be wrong.

You can report a deck, a card or a question from the app (Report). The report sends only the identifier of the generation and the reason you pick, not your notes or the optional details you type (those stay on your phone). Questions or complaints: pedalup180@gmail.com.

Age

Quizloom is for people aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If the Play Age Signals library says an account belongs to a person under 18, the AI features and purchases are turned off, so no notes, photos or identifiers are sent to our server or to the AI provider; studying, importing and backups keep working. If a parent has not approved a change yet, purchases are paused. The age answer is used at that moment and is not stored.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can view, export, back up and delete it from Settings, or by uninstalling the app. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to pedalup180@gmail.com with any question; since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side (the AI server keeps no copy of your notes).

Changes

We may update this policy. The new date appears at the top. Important changes are announced in the app or in Google Play.
