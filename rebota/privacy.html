Rebota Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026 · Leer en español · Rebota

Rebota (the app) is an interval timer, rest timer and strength log for Android. This policy explains what happens with your information. In short: everything stays on your phone.

What the app stores

Rebota stores the following only on your phone, inside the app:

  • Your workouts: dates, exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, effort, rest times, and the routines and custom exercises you create.
  • Your settings: language, units, sounds, vibration and alerts.
  • Optional answers in the questionnaire About you: an age range, sex, body weight and any discomfort in the knee, back or shoulder. Answering is always your choice, and the app works without them.
  • Optional body weight, used only to show an estimate of calories.
  • Optional body weight and body measurements (neck, chest, waist, hips, arm and thigh) that you add yourself in Progress, each with its date. Nothing asks for them: the list is empty until you add something.
  • Technical records for the free trial and your plan: the day the trial started and the last purchase status confirmed by Google Play.

What we collect

Nothing. Rebota has no account, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never receive your workouts, your answers or anything about you. Everything except purchases works without an internet connection. The app does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Health and fitness information

Some of what you can enter is about your body or your health: age range, sex, weight, body measurements and discomforts. It is used only on your phone, to order and adjust the suggestions (for example, choosing exercise variants that do not bother a sore knee), to show a calorie estimate if you turn it on and to draw the chart of the body weight and measurements you choose to add. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, never sold and never used for advertising. Before you answer, the app tells you this, and you can skip any question.

Rebota is for general fitness. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. See also the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy below.

Purchases and subscriptions

Rebota includes a free trial that needs no card. After that you can choose a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase through Google Play billing. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we never see your card or payment details. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in Google Play, and you keep access until the end of the paid period. The interval timer and the rest timer stay free, and you can always view, export and back up your sets.

The Google Play libraries used for billing and for the review window may send technical information to Google, as described in the Google Play terms. Rebota adds no personal data to it.

Sharing, exports and backups

Your data leaves your phone only when you choose to send it:

  • Backup file and CSV export: created only when you tap them, and saved or shared where you choose.
  • Share the month and Recommend Rebota: they only open the share menu of your phone with a short message (and a link to the app). Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it. Your log is never included in a recommendation.
  • Android backup: if it is turned on in your phone, Android may copy your log, settings and trial date to your personal Google backup, under Google terms. The workout in progress, the rest timer and the last result from Google Play are not included.
  • Rate on Google Play: Google Play shows its own review window. Rebota receives no personal data from it.

Permissions

  • Vibration: for the alerts.
  • Exact alarms: so the end of a rest sounds with the screen off. You allow it in Android settings; without it the screen stays on during the rest.
  • Notifications: for the rest countdown and the workout notification. You choose when to allow them.
  • Keep awake (wake lock): a few seconds at most, only to play the alert when a rest ends.
  • Internet, network status and Google Play billing: used only by Google Play to show prices, process purchases and show the review window.
  • Voice: spoken alerts use the voice engine already on your phone. Rebota does not record audio.

Deleting your data

In Settings, section Sets, the button Erase my answers removes your age range, sex, weight and discomforts from the questionnaire, and the weight used for the calorie estimate (your goal, level, days and place stay). It also cleans them from the internal copies of your log kept on the phone. In Progress, Body weight and measurements, you can delete one entry at a time, or use the button Delete all my body entries, which also cleans the internal copies of your log kept on the phone. You can also delete routines, sessions and custom exercises inside the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything stored on the phone. Backup and export files you saved yourself stay where you put them, and a copy in your Google backup is managed from your Google account.

Age

Rebota is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

If you are in the European Union or elsewhere

Because Rebota does not collect or receive your personal data, we hold nothing that we could access, correct or delete for you; you do all of that on your phone. For any question about privacy, write to the contact below.

Accessibility

If something in Rebota is hard to use with your phone accessibility tools, write to us and we will work on it.

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This section applies to consumer health data as defined by the Washington My Health My Data Act and by the Nevada consumer health data law. It can be read on its own.

Categories we handle (only on your phone): age range, sex, body weight, body measurements (neck, chest, waist, hips, arm, thigh) that you add yourself, discomfort in the knee, back or shoulder, and your workout history (sets, weights, effort) with the optional calorie estimate.

Why: to order and adjust exercise suggestions, to show the total load in bodyweight exercises, to show your progress, to draw the chart of your body weight and measurements if you add them, and to estimate calories if you turn it on. Nothing else.

Where it comes from: only from what you type or tap in the app.

Who it is shared with: nobody. We do not receive it, we do not sell it, and no third party can see it. It leaves your phone only when you choose to export or share it.

Sale and advertising: we do not sell consumer health data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use geofencing.

Consent: before asking for any of this information, the app explains what is saved, that it stays on your phone and what it is for. Answering is optional, and you can withdraw your consent at any time by erasing your answers or deleting your body entries.

Your rights: you can see, change and delete this data yourself in the app (Settings, Sets, Erase my answers; Progress, Body weight and measurements, Delete all my body entries) or by uninstalling the app. Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing to request from us, but you may write to us with any question or complaint. You may also contact the attorney general of your state.
