Roomvault Privacy Policy

Version 1, October 2026. Roomvault is a home inventory app, for adults 18 and older, made by Amg Up.

The short version

Your list and your photos stay on your phone. Roomvault has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We cannot see your data, and we never sell or share it.

What Roomvault keeps (only on your phone)

In the private storage of the app, only what you type, photograph or choose:

  • Your inventory: your rooms, your items (name, category, quantity, brand, model, serial number, where and when you bought it, prices, condition and notes), your room walk-throughs and your claims.
  • Your photos: the photos of your items, labels, receipts and rooms, made smaller to save space. Roomvault removes the location from every photo. Your original photos stay where they were.
  • Your home details: the name or address of your home, the people who live there, and your insurance company, agent and policy number if you choose to type them. This is sensitive information: it stays on your phone and in the files you export.
  • Your settings: language, currency, paper size, reminders, the date your free trial started, plus a few internal counters that decide when to show a card. The counters never leave the phone.

What Roomvault does not keep: your location, contacts, card numbers or any account. Roomvault never asks for them.

When it leaves your phone

Only when you do it: when you save a backup (a ZIP file), make a PDF or a spreadsheet file (CSV), or send something with the share menu, it goes to the app or place you choose (for example a cloud drive, email or a message app), and the rules of that place apply from there. A backup is not encrypted: keep it in a private place. If Android backup is on in your phone, Android may keep a copy of your list and settings, without photos, in your own Google account; you control that in your phone's settings, and we cannot read it. If you move to a new phone and choose to copy your apps and data directly from the old one, Android may also move your photos from phone to phone.

Purchases

Roomvault Pro (a subscription or a one-time purchase) is bought through Google Play, under Google's own privacy policy. Roomvault only learns whether Pro is active; it never sees your card. To make purchases work, the Google Play library connects to Google and may send technical data about the purchase and the phone; it never receives your list or your photos. Pro comes only from Google Play, never from a backup file.

Permissions

Notifications: only for the reminders you turn on; they never show the names or the values of your things. Restart receiver: sets your reminders again when the phone restarts. Google Play purchases and internet: used only for prices and purchases. Photos come from the camera app or the photo picker of Android, and a backup is saved or opened with the file picker of Android, so Roomvault needs no camera, photo, video or storage permission. Roomvault does not use location or contacts.

Recommending the app

The button to recommend Roomvault prepares a message with a link to the app. You choose whether to send it, and it never includes your list. The link to rate Roomvault only opens a Google Play page.

Deleting your data

Use "Delete all data" in Settings (after a warning, it asks you to type DELETE), or uninstall the app, and everything is removed from your phone. Backups, PDFs and files you saved yourself stay where you put them; delete them there. Because we do not have your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Adults only

Roomvault is for adults 18 and older. It is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with its date.
