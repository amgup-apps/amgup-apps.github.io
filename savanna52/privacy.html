Privacy policy

Savanna 52 (app.savanna52) · Last updated: October 26, 2026

Savanna 52 collects no data. There is no account, no analytics and no ads. Your rolls, your ledger and your settings stay on your phone.

The month and year of birth that you give in the age check never leave the phone.

A file you export or back up goes only where you choose to save it. If you recommend the app, the message goes through the app you pick, and only after you tap Send.

Pro purchases are handled by Google Play. Google Play Billing, a library of Google inside the app, talks to Google Play to show the prices and to confirm what you bought, and the privacy policy of Google applies to that. This is why the app has network permission. The app itself sends nothing anywhere.

To delete your data, clear the data of the app in the settings of Android or uninstall it. We cannot see your data, so we cannot delete it for you.
