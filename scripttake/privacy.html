Script Take Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Leer en español

Who we are

Script Take is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means Amg Up. Privacy contact: pedalup180@gmail.com.

Short version

Script Take is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. It has no account, no servers for your data, no ads and no analytics. What you write stays on your phone.

Your videos are saved in the gallery of your phone; a video, a backup file or a script you export leaves your phone only when you save or share it yourself. The camera and microphone are used only to record on your phone. Payments are handled by Google Play.

What Script Take stores on your phone

You read your script near the camera while you record. Your videos are saved in the gallery of your phone (Movies/Script Take), with no watermark. Script Take stores your scripts, your settings, the list of your takes and their small preview pictures, and the date you first opened the app. It lives in the private storage of the app. We do not receive it.

What leaves your phone only when you choose

A video you share, a backup file or a .txt file with your scripts that you save or share with the Android menu. You pick where it goes. The message you send when you recommend the app to a friend goes through the app you choose (for example WhatsApp), not through us.

Android automatic backup

If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may copy the app files to your Google account, encrypted. This is a setting of your phone and Google, not of Script Take. It never includes purchases or an unfinished draft.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. Script Take only learns from Google Play whether Pro is active. The Google Play Billing library talks to Google for that; Script Take does not send your scripts or videos.

Age signals

When the app opens and before a purchase, the app may ask Google Play, through the official Play Age Signals library, whether the account belongs to an adult. The answer is used at that moment and is not stored or sent anywhere by Script Take.

Permissions

Camera and microphone (CAMERA and RECORD_AUDIO): asked the first time you tap Record, used only while the recording screen is open to show you and record your video and voice. The video is made on your phone and saved in your gallery; nothing is sent to us or to anyone. Storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): only on Android 7, 8 and 9, to put your videos in the gallery; newer Android versions need no storage permission. Internet and network state: used by the Google Play libraries for purchases. Script Take does not ask for location, contacts or notifications, and Rehearse works without any permission.

Adults only

Script Take is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can view, export, back up and delete it from Settings, or by uninstalling the app. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to pedalup180@gmail.com with any question; since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new date appears at the top. Important changes are announced in the app or in Google Play.

Contact

Questions about privacy: pedalup180@gmail.com
