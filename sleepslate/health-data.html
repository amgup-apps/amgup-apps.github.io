SleepSlate: Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Version 3. Last updated October 27, 2026. This policy is for the laws of Washington, Nevada and other states about consumer health data.

What health data we keep

Only what you type or tap. Your nights: the times you got into bed, turned the lights out, woke up and got out of bed; how long it took to fall asleep; how many times and how long you were awake; what woke you up (bathroom, too hot or cold, pain or discomfort, noise or light, a child or someone you care for, a partner, home or pet, a racing mind, getting up by choice, a bad dream); how you slept; naps; caffeine and alcohol; whether it was a work or school day or a day off; and your notes. If you fill in For my visit: what bothers you most; how long it has been going on; how sleep affects your day (tired, mood, focus or memory, work or school, driving); things you or someone else noticed (loud snoring, waking up choking or short of breath, someone seeing you stop breathing, nodding off while driving, being very sleepy during the day, an urge to move your legs at night, acting out dreams, headaches when you wake up); your work hours; whether you have a baby or young kids, care for an adult family member or many people live in your home; and the questions you want to ask your doctor. The name you type for the PDF is kept too. If you set the date of your doctor visit, that date is kept too, and whether you want a reminder the evening before. SleepSlate also keeps your settings and the trash.

Where it comes from

Only from you. SleepSlate does not read data from other apps, watches or sensors.

Why

To show your diary, calculate your averages and sleep efficiency, remind you each morning, count the nights left before your doctor visit and remind you the evening before if you set its date, and make the PDF you choose to share, with the For my visit page if you filled it in.

Where it is kept

On your phone. SleepSlate has no account and no servers. If the backup of your phone is on, your phone keeps an encrypted copy in your own backup account; we cannot read it. You can turn that backup off in the settings of your phone.

Who we share it with

No one. We do not sell it. A PDF, a backup file or a spreadsheet (CSV) leaves your phone only when you share, print or save it yourself, to the place you choose.

No ads, no analytics

SleepSlate has no ads and no analytics. Purchases go through Google Play, which receives only what it needs for the purchase, never your diary.

Your rights

You can see, change and delete your data in the app at any time (Settings > Delete all my data), or by uninstalling the app. Because we do not have your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with its date, and the app will show a short notice.
