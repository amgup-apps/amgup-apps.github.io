SleepSlate Privacy Policy

Version 6. Last updated November 9, 2026. SleepSlate is a sleep diary for adults 18 and older.

The short version

Your diary stays on your phone. SleepSlate has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We cannot see your diary, and we never sell or share it.

What SleepSlate keeps

Only what you type or tap. Your nights: the times you got into bed, turned the lights out, woke up and got out of bed; how long it took to fall asleep; how many times and how long you were awake; what woke you up (bathroom, too hot or cold, pain or discomfort, noise or light, a child or someone you care for, a partner, home or pet, a racing mind, getting up by choice, a bad dream); how you slept; naps; caffeine and alcohol; whether it was a work or school day or a day off; and your notes. If you fill in For my visit: what bothers you most; how long it has been going on; how sleep affects your day (tired, mood, focus or memory, work or school, driving); things you or someone else noticed (loud snoring, waking up choking or short of breath, someone seeing you stop breathing, nodding off while driving, being very sleepy during the day, an urge to move your legs at night, acting out dreams, headaches when you wake up); your work hours; whether you have a baby or young kids, care for an adult family member or many people live in your home; and the questions you want to ask your doctor. The name you type for the PDF is kept too. If you set the date of your doctor visit, that date is kept too, and whether you want a reminder the evening before. SleepSlate also keeps your settings and the trash. All of it stays in the private storage of the app on your phone.

When it leaves your phone

Only when you do it: when you share or print a PDF, or save a backup file or a spreadsheet (CSV), it goes to the app or place you choose. The PDF has your nights and, if you filled it in, For my visit. The spreadsheet has your nights. The backup file has everything in your diary. If the backup of your phone is on, Android keeps an encrypted copy in your own backup account; we cannot read it.

Purchases

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google Play receives what it needs for the purchase under its own privacy policy; it never receives your diary. To make purchases work, the Google Play library connects to Google and may send technical data about the purchase and the phone.

Permissions

Notifications, only if you allow them: the morning reminder, the reminder before your visit (if you turn them on), while a sound plays a notification with the time left and a Stop button, and two notices a few days before your 30 free days end (no health information in them). Restart, to set the reminder again after the phone restarts. Foreground service for media playback, only while a sound you started is playing: it keeps the sound going with the screen off and shows the notification with the time it stops and a control to stop it. The sounds are made on your phone; SleepSlate does not use the microphone, and the sounds save nothing about you. Fingerprint, only if you turn on the lock. Internet, used only by Google Play: for purchases and for the review window.

Consumer health data

Your diary is health information about you. The Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, a separate page, explains what we keep, why, and your rights.

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom

The person responsible for this app (the controller) is its independent developer, Amg Up, who you can reach at pedalup180@gmail.com. SleepSlate does not collect or transfer personal data: your diary stays on your phone, so there is nothing we could access, correct, delete or hand over for you. You exercise those rights directly in the app: you can read, export, change and delete your diary at any time, and uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Purchases are processed by Google Play under its own terms and privacy policy. If you think your rights were not respected, you can complain to the data protection authority of your country.

Accessibility

SleepSlate follows the text size and dark mode of your phone, works with TalkBack and respects the remove animations setting. If something does not work for you, write to pedalup180@gmail.com and we will try to fix it.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all my data deletes everything from this phone. Uninstalling the app also deletes it. Because we do not have your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Adults only

SleepSlate is for adults 18 and older. It is not meant for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with its date, and the app will show a short notice.
