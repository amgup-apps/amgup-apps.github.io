What SleepSlate is

SleepSlate is a sleep diary you fill in yourself, for adults 18 and older. The numbers come from what you type; the phone does not record your sleep by itself.

Free days and Pro

The first 30 days are free, with everything on and no card. After them, logging new nights needs Pro: a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase, through Google Play. Seeing, editing, the PDF, backup, export and delete stay free. Google Play handles payments, renewals, cancellations and refunds under its own terms. You can cancel a subscription in Google Play at any time; buying Lifetime does not cancel a subscription.
