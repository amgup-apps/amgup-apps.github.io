Stars Overhead Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Leer en español

Who we are

Stars Overhead is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means Amg Up. Privacy contact: pedalup180@gmail.com.

Short version

Stars Overhead is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. It has no account, no servers for your data, no ads and no analytics. Your places, your log and your settings stay on your phone.

Your location is optional, approximate (rounded to about 1 km), used only on your phone and never sent. The app downloads public data from NOAA and CelesTrak; those requests are the same for everyone and carry no data about you.

A backup, CSV or PDF file, or a share card, leaves your phone only when you save or share it yourself. Payments are handled by Google Play.

What Stars Overhead stores on your phone

You see what is in the sky tonight from your places, get the alerts you choose and keep a log of what you saw. Stars Overhead stores your places, the things you mark as seen, your notes and settings. It lives in the private storage of the app. We do not receive it.

Location

Location is optional. If you choose to use your location, Android asks you for approximate location (never precise and never in the background). The app reads it once, while the app is open, rounds it to about 1 km and saves it on your phone as one of your places. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can also pick a city from the list inside the app, which works without the internet.

Internet and public data

Stars Overhead downloads public data with no data about you: the space weather forecast (Kp index) from the NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center, when you open Tonight or the aurora screen, or when the aurora alert you turned on checks every 3 hours, and satellite orbit data from CelesTrak, at most once a day when you open Passes, the map or Tonight.

These requests are the same for everyone and never include your location or anything you saved. Like any website, those servers can see the IP address of the connection. Everything else, including all the sky calculations, works on your phone without the internet. NOAA does not endorse this app.

Alerts and sensors

Alerts are set up on your phone for what you turn on (for example a good night to observe or a space station pass), with the notifications permission and, if you allow it, exact alarms. They do not go through any server.

The map can use the compass and motion sensors of your phone to follow where you point it. Sensor readings are used at that moment and are not saved or sent.

What leaves your phone only when you choose

A backup file, a CSV or PDF of your log, or a share card that you save or share with the Android menu. You pick where it goes. Share cards never include your coordinates or your notes. The message you send when you recommend the app to a friend goes through the app you choose, not through us.

Android automatic backup

If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may copy the app files to your Google account, encrypted. This is a setting of your phone and Google, not of Stars Overhead. It never includes purchases.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. Stars Overhead only learns from Google Play whether Pro is active. The Google Play Billing library talks to Google for that; Stars Overhead does not send your log or your places.

Age signals

Before a purchase the app may ask Google Play, through the Play Age Signals library from Google, about the age range of the account. The answer is used at that moment and is not stored or sent anywhere by Stars Overhead.

Permissions

Approximate location (optional, once, with the app open), notifications and exact alarms (only for the alerts you turn on), start at boot (to put your alerts back after a restart), and internet and network state (public data from NOAA and CelesTrak, and purchases). Stars Overhead does not ask for precise or background location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Children

Stars Overhead is for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, and the app does not collect personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can view, export, back up and delete it from Settings, or by uninstalling the app. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to pedalup180@gmail.com with any question; since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new date appears at the top. Important changes are announced in the app or in Google Play.

Contact
