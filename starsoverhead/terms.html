3. Estimates, alerts and safety

Stars Overhead is a guide for looking at the sky. It is not professional or scientific advice.

(a) Times, directions and positions are approximate calculations; satellite passes also depend on how old the orbit data is. (b) Aurora forecasts come from NOAA and can be wrong. (c) Alerts depend on your phone and its settings, and can arrive late or not at all. (d) Never look at the Sun, not even during a partial or annular eclipse, without filters that meet the ISO 12312-2 standard; the app is not a tool for looking at the Sun. (e) Do not use the app for navigation. (f) When you go out at night, take care of yourself, respect private property and follow the rules of the place.
