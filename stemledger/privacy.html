Stemledger Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Leer en español

Who we are

Stemledger is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means Amg Up. Privacy contact: pedalup180@gmail.com.

Short version

Stemledger is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. It has no account, no servers for your data, no ads and no analytics. What you write stays on your phone.

A backup or CSV file leaves your phone only when you save or share it yourself. Payments are handled by Google Play.

What Stemledger stores on your phone

You write down events, clients and arrangements, and the app calculates stem costs, prices, deposits and reports on your phone. Stemledger stores your events and their payments, your clients (name, phone and notes), your arrangements, stem recipes, flower prices and photos, your business details and logo, your weekly capacity, your theme and language, and the date you first opened the app. It lives in the private storage of the app. We do not receive it.

What leaves your phone only when you choose

A backup file or a CSV file that you save or share with the Android menu; a quote, receipt or PDF you share; and the WhatsApp message to a client that you send yourself from WhatsApp. You pick where it goes. The names and phone numbers of your clients are written by you and stay on your phone; you are responsible for using them only for your orders. The message you send when you recommend the app to a friend goes through the app you choose (for example WhatsApp), not through us.

What the app can share when you decide

When you share something (a proposal, receipt or PDF (it may include photos you added), the shopping list and the message to a client), it leaves your phone only through the app you choose and only to the person you choose. We do not receive it and the app keeps no copy outside your phone.

Android automatic backup

If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may copy the app files to your Google account, encrypted. This is a setting of your phone and Google, not of Stemledger. It never includes purchases or an unfinished draft.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. Stemledger only learns from Google Play whether Pro is active. The Google Play Billing library talks to Google for that; Stemledger does not send your data.

Permissions

Internet and network state: used by the Google Play libraries for purchases. Stemledger does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or your files.

Adults only

Stemledger is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can view, export, back up and delete it from Settings, or by uninstalling the app. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to pedalup180@gmail.com with any question; since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new date appears at the top. Important changes are announced in the app or in Google Play.

Contact

Questions about privacy: pedalup180@gmail.com
