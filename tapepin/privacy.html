Privacy Policy

TapePin · November 9, 2026

TapePin, made by Amg Up, keeps on this phone what you write about your clients: names, phone numbers, addresses for home visits, notes, measurements with their dates, photos (of the client, the design, the fabric, and the windows or rooms you measure), orders and payments.

Nothing is sent to the developer. There is no account, no ads and no analytics. The app works without internet. Permissions: notifications, for your reminders (fittings, deliveries and payments); the restart permission, so that your reminders are set again when the phone is turned on; and fingerprint, only if you turn on the lock, so that Android checks your fingerprint (TapePin never receives or stores it).

Photos are kept in a private folder of the app, without the GPS location (the EXIF data is removed). TapePin has no face recognition. Photos of a window or room are of the place only, never of people.

The Android backup to the cloud (Google) carries only the texts, and only when the phone encrypts it (with a screen lock); never the photos. When you move to a new phone with a cable or wifi, Android can also move the photos, without uploading them to any server. The backup file (.zip) is saved by you wherever you choose, or automatically to a folder you pick once in Settings (TapePin keeps the last 4 copies there and only touches its own copy files; it sends nothing to any server, and if that folder is in a cloud service such as Google Drive, that service stores the copies under its own terms).

When you share by WhatsApp, PDF or printing, you do it; from there the rules of that app apply.

You ask your clients for permission, above all for photos and for minors, and you delete their data when they ask.

TapePin is not for children. The data of minors is written by an adult with the permission of a parent or guardian, and minors never have photos of the person (no profile and no fitting photos); only of the fabric, the design and the garment on the hanger.

If you pick a client from your contacts, only that name and number are copied into the app.

How to delete: delete a client (it goes to the trash for 30 days, then it is gone), delete it now from the trash, or uninstall the app. Purchases are processed by Google Play.

Consumer health data: the app does not collect consumer health data. Measurements stay on your phone and are never sent to the developer.

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the app shows the new text.
