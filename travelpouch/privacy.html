Travel Pouch Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Leer en español

Who we are

Travel Pouch is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means Amg Up. Privacy contact: pedalup180@gmail.com.

Short version

Travel Pouch is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. It has no account, no servers for your data, no ads and no analytics. Your trips, files and documents stay on your phone.

The only connection the app makes on its own downloads a public table of reference exchange rates, the same for everyone; it carries nothing about you. A backup, CSV file, trip PDF, group summary or image leaves your phone only when you save or share it yourself, and it never includes your documents unless you choose to add them. Payments are handled by Google Play.

What Travel Pouch stores on your phone

You plan and keep your trips: bookings with their tickets, packing lists, group expenses in any currency, travel documents and phrases, all on your phone. Travel Pouch stores your trips, bookings, the files and photos you add, packing lists, expenses, the names of the people you travel with, your travel documents and settings. It lives in the private storage of the app, together with the files and photos you add. We do not receive it.

Exchange rates: the only connection

To convert currencies, the app downloads a public table of reference exchange rates from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev) and, if that fails, the daily reference rates of the European Central Bank (ecb.europa.eu). It asks only while the app is open, at most every 15 minutes. The request is always the same full table, so it carries nothing about you, your trips or your expenses. Frankfurter says its API does not collect or log personal data or IP addresses. Without internet, the app uses the last table it saved, with its date.

Files, photos and what you share to the app

You add files and photos with the Android file or photo picker, the system camera, or by sending them to the app with "Share" or "Open with". They are copied into the private storage of the app and stay there. The app needs no camera, photos or storage permission.

What leaves your phone only when you choose

A backup file, a CSV file, the trip PDF, the group summary or the share image, when you save or share it with the Android menu. You pick where it goes. They never include your documents unless you switch them on and confirm; the full backup does include them, because it is your own copy of everything.

"Share a copy" of a document sends it only after you confirm. "Report a phrase" opens your own email app with a message you can read before you send it. The message you send when you recommend the app to a friend goes through the app you choose (for example WhatsApp), not through us. Maps, the phone dialer and your email app open only when you tap them.

Android automatic backup

If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may copy the text data of the app (trips, bookings, packing lists, expenses, document details and settings) to your Google account, encrypted. It never includes the files and photos you added, purchases or an unfinished draft. This is a setting of your phone and Google, not of Travel Pouch.

Lock, screen and reminders

If you turn on the lock for your papers, the app asks for the screen lock of your phone (fingerprint, face or PIN) through Android. The app never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN. While a document is open, the app blocks screenshots and hides it from the recent apps screen.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your phone; nothing is sent to a server. "Speak" uses the text-to-speech voice of your phone, set by Android; Travel Pouch does not send the phrase anywhere.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. Travel Pouch only learns from Google Play whether Pro is active. The Google Play Billing library talks to Google for that; Travel Pouch does not send your trips or documents.

Age signals

When the app opens and before a purchase, the app may ask Google Play, through the official Play Age Signals library, whether the account belongs to an adult. The answer is used at that moment and is not stored or sent anywhere by Travel Pouch.

Permissions

Internet and network state: only to download the public exchange-rate tables (Frankfurter and, if it fails, the European Central Bank) and for Google Play purchases; the rate request carries no data about you. Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): only for the reminders about what you saved, asked after your first trip. Restart receiver (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): puts your reminders back after the phone restarts. Biometric (USE_BIOMETRIC): to unlock your papers with the screen lock of your phone. Files and photos go through the system pickers or the system camera. Travel Pouch does not ask for location, contacts, its own camera, microphone or storage permissions.

Adults only

Travel Pouch is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can view, export, back up and delete it from Settings, or by uninstalling the app. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to pedalup180@gmail.com with any question; since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new date appears at the top. Important changes are announced in the app or in Google Play.
