TrickPace Privacy Policy

Version 1, November 2026. TrickPace is a 5-minute daily dog training plan with a clicker. Made by Amg Up.

The short version

TrickPace does not collect your data and does not share it. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your dogs, photos and progress stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

In the private storage of the app, TrickPace keeps only what you add or choose: your dogs (name, age group, look, goals, optional photo and house words), your plan and progress for each skill, your training sessions and notes, your reminder choices and your settings. Nothing is uploaded to a server, because TrickPace does not have one.

Photos of your dog

A photo is optional, one per dog. You pick it with the photo picker of Android. TrickPace shrinks it, saves it without location data (EXIF) and never analyzes it. It stays on your phone unless you share a card that includes it.

The clicker

The clicker plays its own sounds and can vibrate. It listens to the volume buttons or a Bluetooth clicker only while the app is open, and keeps the screen on only during a session. TrickPace does not use the microphone or the camera.

The internet

TrickPace uses the internet in only one case: for purchases and the state of your plan with Google Play. The pages of the app itself never use the network. Everything else, including the plan, the lessons, the clicker and your progress, works without internet. The Terms and Privacy links open in your browser.

Sharing, exporting and backups

TrickPace shares a card (an image), a file, an export (CSV), a backup (a ZIP with your photos) or a text message to WhatsApp or another app only when you ask, and it goes where you choose. From there the policy of that app or service applies. Backups and exports are saved where you pick with the file picker of Android, and restoring reads only the file you pick.

Android backup

If you turn on Android automatic backup, Android may save your dogs, progress, sessions, settings and dog photos in your Google account, and only when the backup is encrypted. When you move to a new phone with a cable or phone-to-phone transfer, the same things can travel. This is done by Android with your account, not by us.

Reminders and notifications

TrickPace asks for the notifications permission for the daily training reminder, the reminder 2 days before your free trial ends, and the reminders 30 and 7 days before a yearly renewal. The reminders are set on your phone and set again after a restart or a change of time or time zone. They are not exact alarms.

Rating and recommending

If you choose to rate TrickPace, the review window of Google Play opens and TrickPace never sees your review. Recommend opens the share sheet of your phone with a message and a link, and you choose who gets it.

Purchases

Purchases are made in Google Play, under its terms and privacy policy. TrickPace never sees your card. It only learns from Google Play which plan you have and its dates, and keeps that on your phone so it can unlock Pro.

Permissions

TrickPace asks for: internet and network state and Google Play billing (for purchases), notifications, start after a restart (to set the reminders again) and vibration (for the clicker). It does not ask for storage, camera, microphone, contacts, location or exact alarms.

If the app closes unexpectedly

TrickPace can show an error screen with a Share button. The error text goes nowhere unless you tap Share, and you choose where it goes.

Children

TrickPace is for adults, 18 or older, and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Children can train a dog together with an adult. Plans are bought through Google Play under its rules.

Deleting your data

In Settings, Delete all data erases your dogs, progress and sessions from your phone. You can also delete one dog or one session (with Undo for a few seconds), or uninstall TrickPace to remove everything it keeps. Files you already saved or shared somewhere else stay there.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: pedalup180@gmail.com.
