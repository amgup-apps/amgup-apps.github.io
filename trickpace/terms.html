Using TrickPace

TrickPace is educational content that teaches dog training with rewards. It does not replace a professional dog trainer or a veterinarian. The safety of your dog, of you and of other people is your responsibility. For aggression, strong fear or panic when alone, work with a professional dog trainer or a veterinary behaviorist. Children train with an adult.

No promise of results

Every dog learns at its own pace. TrickPace does not promise any result or any time frame.

Plans and prices

  • Monthly: US$7.99 per month, renews every month until you cancel.

Cancel anytime in Google Play

Cancel in TrickPace Settings > Plan > Manage, which opens your subscriptions in Google Play, or in Google Play > Payments and subscriptions. We remind you 30 and 7 days before a yearly renewal, with a notification; the reminder is a courtesy, so the date in Google Play is the one that counts. Refunds are handled by Google Play, under its rules and the law that applies where you live (for example, the right of withdrawal in the European Union and the United Kingdom).

What Pro unlocks

Pro unlocks the daily plan, the 14-day plans, the potty log, the lessons (except the 6 basic ones: Name game, Marker, Sit, Down, Touch and Come), new training sessions, the guides (except the potty guide), reminders and more than one dog. The clicker, your progress, learned tricks, sharing, house words, safety help, export, backup and restore always stay free. When the 30 free days or a plan end, nothing is deleted: your dogs, sessions and progress stay safe on your phone.
