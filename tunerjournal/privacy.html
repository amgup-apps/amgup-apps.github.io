Tuner Journal Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Leer en español

Who we are

Tuner Journal is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means Amg Up. Privacy contact: pedalup180@gmail.com.

Short version

Tuner Journal is made by Amg Up, an independent developer. It has no account, no servers for your data, no ads and no analytics. Your pieces, recordings, journal and settings stay on your phone.

The microphone listens only while the tuner or the recorder is open on the screen. What it hears is processed on your phone and is never sent.

A recording, a sheet, a report, a share card or a backup file leaves your phone only when you share or save it yourself. Payments are handled by Google Play.

What Tuner Journal stores on your phone

You keep your songs and exercises with their tempo and tuning, tune your instrument, play with the metronome, record takes and keep a practice journal, all on your phone. Tuner Journal stores your pieces, lists, the chord sheets you write or import, photos of your sheet music, the recordings you make, your practice sessions and settings. It lives in the private storage of the app. We do not receive it.

Microphone

Tuner Journal asks for the microphone the first time you open the tuner or tap Record, never when the app opens. The microphone is used only while the tuner or the recorder is on the screen: it closes when you leave that screen or the app, and a recording stops and is saved. There is no background service.

The sound for the tuner is analyzed on your phone at that moment and is not stored. Takes are saved as audio files (M4A) in the private storage of the app. Nothing you play is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

Photos, files and reminders

Photos of your sheet music come from the camera app or the photo picker of your phone, only when you choose them; Tuner Journal does not ask for camera, photo or file permissions. Chord files and backups are opened and saved with the file picker of Android.

If you turn on the practice reminder or the gig countdown, the reminder is set up on your phone with the notifications permission. It does not go through any server. Vibration is used for the metronome and the in-tune signal, if you turn them on.

What leaves your phone only when you choose

A take (M4A), a chord sheet (.cho or PDF), the monthly report (PDF), a share card (image), a CSV of your journal or a backup file that you save or share with the Android menu. You pick where it goes. Share cards never include your notes, lyrics, sheets or recordings. The message you send when you recommend the app to a friend goes through the app you choose, not through us.

Android automatic backup

If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may copy the text data of the app (pieces, lists, journal and settings) to your Google account, encrypted. This is a setting of your phone and Google, not of Tuner Journal. It never includes your recordings, your sheet photos or purchases.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. Tuner Journal only learns from Google Play whether Pro is active. The Google Play Billing library talks to Google for that; Tuner Journal does not send your pieces, recordings or journal.

Age signals

When the app opens and before a purchase, the app may ask Google Play, through the Play Age Signals library from Google, about the age range of the account. The answer is used at that moment and is not stored or sent anywhere by Tuner Journal.

Permissions

Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO: the tuner and takes, only with the app on the screen), notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS: only for the reminders you turn on), start at boot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: to put your reminders back after a restart), vibration (VIBRATE), and internet and network state (only for purchases with Google Play). Tuner Journal does not ask for location, contacts, camera, photos or files, and it uses no exact alarms and no background service.

Children

Tuner Journal is for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, and the app does not collect personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Everything is on your phone: you can view, export, back up and delete it from Settings (Delete all data also deletes your recordings and sheet photos), or by uninstalling the app. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, you can write to pedalup180@gmail.com with any question; since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new date appears at the top. Important changes are announced in the app or in Google Play.

Contact

Questions about privacy: pedalup180@gmail.com
