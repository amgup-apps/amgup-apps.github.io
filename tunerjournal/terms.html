3. Readings, sound and what you add

Tuner Journal is a tool to practice music. It is not professional advice and it does not replace a teacher or an instrument technician.

Tuner readings depend on the microphone of your phone and on the noise around you, and they can be wrong. Before you raise a string a lot, check the note. We are not responsible for strings or instruments that get damaged.

Take care of your hearing: use the metronome and the reference tone at a comfortable volume, especially with headphones.

What you import or write is yours: only add lyrics, chords or sheet music that you have the right to use. The app does not include songs that are under copyright: the sample pieces are in the public domain, and their chords and arrangements were written by us.

If you record other people, ask for their permission first. Recording laws change from place to place.

With Bluetooth headphones the sound arrives late, so the click and the light of the metronome may not match. You can adjust the light in Settings.

Reminders depend on your phone (battery saving, Do Not Disturb and maker settings) and may arrive late or not at all.
